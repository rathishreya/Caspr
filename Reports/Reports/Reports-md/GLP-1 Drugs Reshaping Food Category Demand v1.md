## Executive Summary

The transformative impact of GLP-1 receptor agonists, which gained prominence between 2024 and 2026, is explored, focusing on the shift from a high-growth therapeutic niche to a major consumer market influence. This shift prompted broad implications for sectors beyond healthcare, notably affecting grocery, foodservice, and consumer packaged goods in the U.S. due to high obesity rates and adoption of GLP-1 drugs. The increased use of these drugs influenced food demand, impacting manufacturers, retailers, and investors by altering consumption patterns, appetite, and food preferences, with significant changes noted in food categories like salty snacks and soft drinks.

The expansion of GLP-1 drug use from 2024 to 2026 resulted in significant household penetration, influencing purchasing behaviors, especially in weight management and nutrition-focused categories. By 2030, it is anticipated that GLP-1 user households could represent a significant portion of U.S. food and beverage sales. There is an increased predominance of these drugs among women and middle-aged users, with a noted impact from concentrated household spending. The report underscores the potential for sustained adoption due to expanding medical indications and insurance coverage.

The adoption of GLP-1 is notably altering demand across specific food categories. The decline in demand for low-nutrient, high-frequency foods is contrasted by increased preference for nutrient-dense, satiety-providing products like fresh produce, yogurt, and protein snacks. The shift reflects consumer reallocations rather than overall demand reduction, reshaping market strategies toward premiumized, functional food innovations driven by consumer prioritization of nutritional utility over caloric content.

GLP-1 integration in household systems also prompts spending reallocations toward fitness, apparel, and self-care, while non-users in these households adapt similar dietary changes. Clinical studies align with retail data in showing decreased energy intake and spending patterns, although economic implications suggest strategic transitions in industry portfolios and product offerings to include protein and fiber-rich options. Industry-wide shifts reflect adaptations towards nutrition efficiency, marked by reformulations and new product introductions targeting GLP-1 users.

Projections suggest that GLP-1 could trigger a significant revenue shift in the food and beverage industry, with a potential structural shift observed as GLP-1 use becomes more widespread. Income, demographics, and consumption behavior dynamics are key influencing factors, with industry recommendations focusing on adapting to nutritional utility and market strategies that align with consumer behavior influenced by GLP-1 drug use. The report concludes by emphasizing the importance of aligning industry practices with nutritional frameworks and credible evidence to capture growth and maintain market relevance.

## 1. Introduction and Context

GLP-1 receptor agonists moved from a high growth therapeutic niche into a broad consumer market force between 2024 and 2026, with implications extending beyond healthcare into grocery, foodservice, and consumer packaged goods. In the U.S., adoption accelerated as obesity prevalence remained structurally high, new indications and products expanded the addressable market, and survey evidence showed a sharp rise in both lifetime and current use, creating a credible pathway for measurable changes in food demand and basket composition.[11](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/kff-health-tracking-poll-may-2024-the-publics-use-and-views-of-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=The%20survey%20was%20conducted,and%20in%20Spanish%20%2883%29) [13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=KFF%E2%80%99s%20latest%20Health%20Tracking,points%20from%20May%202024%29) [20](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db508.htm#:~:text=The%20prevalence%20of%20severe,%28Figure%203%2C%20Table%203%29) [15](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-new-medication-chronic-weight-management#:~:text=Food%20and%20Drug%20Administration,and%20increased%20physical%20activity)

This report frames GLP-1 adoption not only as a pharmaceutical story but as a demand reallocation story. The central analytical question is how appetite suppression, altered satiety, and changing food preferences translate into lower demand for some categories and higher demand for others, with consequences for manufacturers, retailers, restaurants, and investors.[32](https://doi.org/10.1038%2Fs41366-024-01500-y#:~:text=Recent%20studies%20investigating%20the,analog%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Ctirzepatide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Cliraglutide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Csemaglutide.%E2%80%9D) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,research%20from%20Circana%20LLC) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,breakfast%20cereals%20and%20bagels)

### 1.1. Overview of GLP-1 drug adoption trajectory in the U.S. (2024-2026)

The U.S. adoption curve steepened materially from 2024 into 2026. In May 2024, KFF found that 12% of U.S. adults had ever used a GLP-1 drug and 6% were current users; by KFF’s 2025 update, 18% reported ever use and 12% reported current use, indicating a doubling in current self-reported use within roughly one year.[11](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/kff-health-tracking-poll-may-2024-the-publics-use-and-views-of-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=The%20survey%20was%20conducted,and%20in%20Spanish%20%2883%29) [13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=KFF%E2%80%99s%20latest%20Health%20Tracking,points%20from%20May%202024%29)

- Clinical access expanded the market: FDA approval of Zepbound for chronic weight management in November 2023 added a major new entrant ahead of the 2024 to 2026 acceleration period, complementing Wegovy’s earlier obesity positioning and broadening prescriber and patient choice.[15](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-approves-new-medication-chronic-weight-management#:~:text=Food%20and%20Drug%20Administration,and%20increased%20physical%20activity) [23](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2024/215256s015lbl.pdf#:~:text=Severe%20Gastrointestinal%20Adverse%20Reactions,Adverse%20Reactions%20%285.6%29%2011%2F2024)
- Structural demand conditions remained favorable: U.S. adult obesity prevalence was 40.3% during August 2021 to August 2023, with severe obesity at 9.4%, leaving a very large clinically relevant population even before considering overweight adults with comorbidities.[20](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db508.htm#:~:text=The%20prevalence%20of%20severe,%28Figure%203%2C%20Table%203%29)
- Coverage remained a constraint but not a stopper: KFF reported that only 1% of ACA Marketplace prescription drug plans covered Wegovy for weight loss in 2024, while employer coverage for GLP-1s used primarily for weight loss was still selective, implying that adoption growth occurred despite meaningful reimbursement friction.[26](https://www.kff.org/affordable-care-act/costly-glp-1-drugs-are-rarely-covered-for-weight-loss-by-marketplace-plans/#:~:text=Affordable%20Care%20Act%20%28ACA%29,approved%20only%20for%20diabetes) [21](https://files.kff.org/attachment/Employer-Health-Benefits-Survey-2024-Annual-Survey-Summary-of-Findings.pdf#:~:text=SOURCE%3A%20KFF%20Employer%20Health%20Benefits%20Survey%2C%202024)
- Consumer market relevance became visible by 2025 to 2026: Circana projected that GLP-1 users would represent 35% of U.S. food and beverage sales by 2030, while NIQ reported that GLP-1 households represented 4.5% of households yet drove 43% of sales growth for products with muscle health claims across consecutive 52-week periods in 2024 and 2025.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,research%20from%20Circana%20LLC) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,breakfast%20cereals%20and%20bagels)

Taken together, the 2024 to 2026 period should be viewed as the transition from early mass awareness to measurable household penetration. For food sector analysis, the key point is not only how many people use GLP-1 drugs, but that adoption reached a scale where scanner data, survey panels, and category growth patterns began to show statistically and commercially meaningful divergence from non-user households.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=KFF%E2%80%99s%20latest%20Health%20Tracking,points%20from%20May%202024%29) [29](https://www.circana.com/post/circana-report-highlights-the-role-of-personalization-in-supporting-glp-1-weight-loss-users#:~:text=The%20Ripple%20Effect%20of,end%20of%20year%20one) [31](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2025/glp-1-consumer-behavior-and-growth-strategies/#:~:text=who%20oversees%20the%20CSF,shop%20and%20make%20purchases)

### 1.2. Mechanism of appetite suppression and its link to food purchasing behavior

GLP-1 drugs influence food demand because they act on the biological drivers of eating, not just on body weight outcomes. Reviews published in 2024 to 2026 describe appetite reduction as arising from central nervous system signaling that lowers hunger and food reward, alongside peripheral effects such as delayed gastric emptying and enhanced satiety, which together reduce meal size, snacking frequency, and desire for energy-dense foods.[38](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41392-024-01931-z#:~:text=GLP-1%20reduces%20appetite%20and,actions%20in%20the%20CNS) [32](https://doi.org/10.1038%2Fs41366-024-01500-y#:~:text=Recent%20studies%20investigating%20the,analog%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Ctirzepatide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Cliraglutide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Csemaglutide.%E2%80%9D) [39](https://doi.org/10.1111/obr.70148#:~:text=A%20crossover%20trial%20investigating,no%20dose-dependent%20effect%20%5B34%5D)

- Appetite pathway: GLP-1 receptor agonists reduce appetite and food intake through CNS-mediated effects on satiety and reward processing, helping explain why users often report less interest in highly palatable foods rather than only smaller portions of the same foods.[38](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41392-024-01931-z#:~:text=GLP-1%20reduces%20appetite%20and,actions%20in%20the%20CNS) [32](https://doi.org/10.1038%2Fs41366-024-01500-y#:~:text=Recent%20studies%20investigating%20the,analog%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Ctirzepatide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Cliraglutide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Csemaglutide.%E2%80%9D)
- Gastrointestinal pathway: Delayed gastric emptying and prolonged fullness can compress eating occasions and increase sensitivity to rich, greasy, or large meals, which is commercially relevant for snacks, desserts, fried foods, and large-format restaurant occasions.[39](https://doi.org/10.1111/obr.70148#:~:text=A%20crossover%20trial%20investigating,no%20dose-dependent%20effect%20%5B34%5D) [37](https://jamanetwork.com/journals/jama/article-abstract/2821290?guestaccesskey=b75db787-f443-41ef-9faa-108eadf9931e#:~:text=such%20as%20phentermine-topiramate%20and,nausea%29%20varies%20by%20medication)
- Behavioral translation: A 2024 narrative review concluded that available studies generally show decreased short-term appetite and food intake under treatment, while retail and shopper datasets in 2025 to 2026 indicate lower overall food and beverage purchasing but stronger orientation toward protein, digestive health, and nutrient-dense products.[34](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2667368124000238#:~:text=100121%23%20Dietary%20intake%20by,discussion%20of%20research%20needs) [29](https://www.circana.com/post/circana-report-highlights-the-role-of-personalization-in-supporting-glp-1-weight-loss-users#:~:text=The%20Ripple%20Effect%20of,end%20of%20year%20one) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,breakfast%20cereals%20and%20bagels)

The link to purchasing behavior is therefore mechanistic as well as observational. If consumers feel full sooner, experience less craving for sugar and fat, and become more intentional about nutrient density, the expected basket effect is lower unit demand in indulgent and convenience categories and relatively better resilience in protein, produce, yogurt, hydration, supplements, and smaller portion formats, a pattern increasingly reflected in industry scanner and survey evidence by 2025 and 2026.[32](https://doi.org/10.1038%2Fs41366-024-01500-y#:~:text=Recent%20studies%20investigating%20the,analog%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Ctirzepatide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Cliraglutide%2C%E2%80%9D%20%E2%80%9Csemaglutide.%E2%80%9D) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,research%20from%20Circana%20LLC) [31](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2025/glp-1-consumer-behavior-and-growth-strategies/#:~:text=who%20oversees%20the%20CSF,shop%20and%20make%20purchases)

## 2. Adoption Scale and Household Penetration

GLP-1 adoption moved from an emerging therapeutic trend to a measurable consumer market force between 2024 and 2026. For this report section, the most decision-useful lens is not only individual prescription growth, but the speed at which use has translated into household penetration, demographic concentration, and a credible pathway to materially larger influence on food and beverage demand by 2030.[11](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/kff-health-tracking-poll-may-2024-the-publics-use-and-views-of-glp-1-drugs/) [13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals)

The evidence base points to three linked conclusions. First, self-reported U.S. adult use rose sharply from 2024 into 2025, with 2026 survey data indicating continued expansion. Second, household penetration is already high enough to affect category-level purchasing patterns, even though user households remain a minority. Third, the addressable population remains very large because obesity and overweight prevalence still far exceed current treatment penetration, implying that adoption can continue rising even under imperfect insurance coverage and affordability constraints.[74](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx#:~:text=D.C.%20%E2%80%94%20The%20percentage,names%20Mounjaro%20and%20Zepbound%29%3F%E2%80%9D) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition) [56](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/hestat/hestat111.htm#:~:text=Prevalence%20of%20childhood%20and,the%20United%20States%2C%202011%E2%80%932012) [62](https://www.healthsystemtracker.org/brief/how-many-adults-with-private-health-insurance-could-use-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=Over%202%20in%205,Drug%20Administration%20%28FDA%29%20indications)

### 2.1. Current U.S. adult usage rates and growth from 2024 to 2026

KFF provides the clearest like-for-like benchmark for all-purpose GLP-1 use across U.S. adults. In May 2024, 12% of adults said they had ever used a GLP-1 drug and 6% said they were current users; by KFF’s November 2025 update, ever use had risen to 18% and current use to 12%, implying a doubling in current self-reported use in roughly 18 months.[11](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/kff-health-tracking-poll-may-2024-the-publics-use-and-views-of-glp-1-drugs/) [13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions)

Gallup’s July 7, 2026 survey, while narrower because it focuses on weight loss use rather than all indications, confirms that growth continued into 2026. Gallup found that 15% of U.S. adults had used GLP-1 medications for weight loss at some point and 11% were currently taking them for that purpose, up from 6% and 3% respectively in 2024.[74](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx#:~:text=D.C.%20%E2%80%94%20The%20percentage,names%20Mounjaro%20and%20Zepbound%29%3F%E2%80%9D)

- Growth pattern: The 2024 to 2026 period is best characterized as a transition from early mass awareness to early mass adoption, with current use moving from mid-single digits to low double digits in national surveys.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions) [74](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx#:~:text=D.C.%20%E2%80%94%20The%20percentage,names%20Mounjaro%20and%20Zepbound%29%3F%E2%80%9D)
- Addressable market depth: CDC estimates show 40.3% of U.S. adults had obesity in August 2021 to August 2023, 9.7% had severe obesity, and another 31.7% were overweight, leaving a very large clinically relevant pool relative to current treatment penetration.[56](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/hestat/hestat111.htm#:~:text=Prevalence%20of%20childhood%20and,the%20United%20States%2C%202011%E2%80%932012) [20](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db508.htm#:~:text=Obesity%20is%20a%20chronic,and%20certain%20cancers%20%281%29)
- Eligibility versus actual use: Peterson-KFF estimated that more than 2 in 5 adults under age 65 with private insurance could be eligible for GLP-1 drugs under current FDA indications, underscoring how far actual use still sits below theoretical demand.[62](https://www.healthsystemtracker.org/brief/how-many-adults-with-private-health-insurance-could-use-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=Over%202%20in%205,Drug%20Administration%20%28FDA%29%20indications)
- Constraint set: Coverage remains a gating factor rather than a hard ceiling. KFF’s 2025 employer survey reported that about 19% of large firms offering health benefits covered GLP-1 drugs for weight loss, up from 18% in 2024, with much higher coverage among the very largest employers.[72](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/2025-employer-health-benefits-survey/#:~:text=The%20sample%20for%20the,ten%20or%20more%20employees)

For food demand analysis, the key implication is scale. Once current use reaches roughly one in eight adults in broad population polling, the effect is no longer confined to specialty health cohorts and becomes visible in mainstream grocery, mass retail, and restaurant demand data.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions) [74](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx#:~:text=D.C.%20%E2%80%94%20The%20percentage,names%20Mounjaro%20and%20Zepbound%29%3F%E2%80%9D)

### 2.2. Household-level penetration and demographic profile of GLP-1 users

Household penetration is commercially more important than individual prevalence because food is purchased at the household level. NIQ reported in its 2026 health and wellness analysis that GLP-1 users represented 4.5% of households in its panel comparison, yet those households drove 43% of sales growth for products carrying muscle health claims across consecutive 52-week periods in 2024 and 2025, showing that even modest penetration can have outsized category effects when spending is concentrated.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition)

Circana’s late 2025 work suggests a much larger household footprint when defined as households containing at least one GLP-1 user. Circana stated in a public 2030 outlook that GLP-1 users are projected to represent 35% of U.S. food and beverage sales by 2030, and a related public presentation cited by search results described GLP-1 users as currently 23% of U.S. households, indicating that household exposure is already materially broader than individual current use rates alone would imply.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals) [79](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NBxKQsMuc-U#:~:text=New%20research%20from%20Circana,beverage%20sales%20by%202030)

- Gender: KFF’s 2025 polling shows current GLP-1 use is higher among women than men, consistent with earlier obesity treatment utilization patterns and with the stronger consumer orientation of women in household grocery decision-making.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions)
- Age: Use is concentrated in prime middle-age cohorts rather than the youngest adults, which matters because these cohorts typically control larger household food budgets and have higher rates of obesity, diabetes, and cardiometabolic risk.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions) [20](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db508.htm#:~:text=Obesity%20is%20a%20chronic,and%20certain%20cancers%20%281%29)
- Health status: KFF found much higher current use among adults with diabetes, obesity or overweight, and heart disease than in the general population, confirming that adoption remains clinically anchored even as it broadens into mainstream consumer behavior.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions)
- Socioeconomic profile: Coverage and affordability skew access toward insured and higher-income households, especially those linked to large employer plans. This creates an early adopter profile that overlaps with higher-spending grocery households, magnifying commercial impact per user household.[72](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/2025-employer-health-benefits-survey/#:~:text=The%20sample%20for%20the,ten%20or%20more%20employees) [62](https://www.healthsystemtracker.org/brief/how-many-adults-with-private-health-insurance-could-use-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=Over%202%20in%205,Drug%20Administration%20%28FDA%29%20indications)
- Education and income interaction: CDC data show obesity prevalence is lower among adults with a bachelor’s degree or more, but the absolute eligible population remains large across all education groups. In practice, higher education and income may reduce clinical prevalence somewhat while increasing treatment access, producing a mixed but commercially attractive user base for premium nutrition categories.[20](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/products/databriefs/db508.htm#:~:text=Obesity%20is%20a%20chronic,and%20certain%20cancers%20%281%29)

The market significance of household penetration lies in spillover. A household with one GLP-1 user often changes basket composition for shared meals, snack inventory, and restaurant ordering, so the commercial footprint expands beyond the treated individual. That is why scanner-based household measures can show meaningful category effects even when national individual use rates still appear moderate.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals)

### 2.3. Projections for adoption through 2030 and their market implications

The most credible 2030 adoption outlooks imply continued expansion rather than saturation. Circana projects that GLP-1 user households will account for 35% of U.S. food and beverage sales by 2030, a forecast that does not require majority household penetration to matter commercially because user households already spend differently and influence broader household purchasing patterns.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals)

Several structural drivers support further adoption through 2030. The eligible population remains far larger than the treated population, employer coverage is gradually widening, and NIQ notes that additional regulatory approvals in conditions such as chronic kidney disease, liver disease, heart failure, and sleep apnea could materially expand the addressable market beyond obesity and diabetes alone.[62](https://www.healthsystemtracker.org/brief/how-many-adults-with-private-health-insurance-could-use-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=Over%202%20in%205,Drug%20Administration%20%28FDA%29%20indications) [72](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/2025-employer-health-benefits-survey/#:~:text=The%20sample%20for%20the,ten%20or%20more%20employees) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition)

- Base case trajectory: If current use remains in the low double digits by 2026 and coverage broadens incrementally, adoption can plausibly move from an early adopter phase to a mainstream chronic care category by 2030. This is an inference from current survey growth, eligibility depth, and expanding payer participation rather than a single published population forecast.[13](https://www.kff.org/public-opinion/kff-health-tracking-poll-prescription-drug-costs-views-on-trump-administration-actions-and-glp-1-use/#:~:text=One%20in%20Eight%20Adults,diagnosed%20with%20chronic%20conditions) [74](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx#:~:text=D.C.%20%E2%80%94%20The%20percentage,names%20Mounjaro%20and%20Zepbound%29%3F%E2%80%9D) [62](https://www.healthsystemtracker.org/brief/how-many-adults-with-private-health-insurance-could-use-glp-1-drugs/#:~:text=Over%202%20in%205,Drug%20Administration%20%28FDA%29%20indications)
- Upside scenario: Faster uptake would likely come from lower net prices, broader employer and public coverage, and label expansion into additional chronic conditions. Under that scenario, food demand effects would diffuse more quickly from premium and health-oriented categories into mass-market center store and foodservice.[72](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/2025-employer-health-benefits-survey/#:~:text=The%20sample%20for%20the,ten%20or%20more%20employees) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition)
- Downside scenario: High discontinuation, reimbursement pullbacks, or persistent affordability barriers could slow the pace of penetration. Even then, the installed base may remain large enough to sustain category-level effects because the highest-spending households are disproportionately represented among early adopters.[73](https://www.kff.org/health-costs/perspectives-from-employers-on-the-costs-and-issues-associated-with-covering-glp-1-agonists-for-weight-loss/#:~:text=Many%20of%20these%20employers,manage%20employer%20health%20benefits) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition)
- Market implication for manufacturers: Portfolio exposure should be assessed by household penetration sensitivity, not just by total category size. Categories dependent on impulse calories, large portions, or frequent snacking face the greatest long-run risk, while protein-dense, nutrient-efficient, and portion-controlled formats gain strategic relevance as penetration rises.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition)
- Market implication for investors: By 2030, GLP-1 exposure is likely to be a recurring screen in food and beverage valuation work, similar to how investors already assess exposure to private label, channel mix, and health and wellness trends. The importance comes less from total user count alone than from the concentration of spending power and the persistence of basket reshaping at the household level.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals) [74](https://news.gallup.com/poll/712157/glp-usage-reaches-new-high.aspx#:~:text=D.C.%20%E2%80%94%20The%20percentage,names%20Mounjaro%20and%20Zepbound%29%3F%E2%80%9D)

In strategic terms, 2030 is close enough that food and beverage companies should treat GLP-1 adoption as a planning variable, not a speculative tail risk. The combination of rising adult use, widening household penetration, and a still underpenetrated eligible population supports a view that GLP-1-driven demand reallocation will remain a live market issue throughout the rest of the decade.[56](https://www.cdc.gov/nchs/data/hestat/hestat111.htm#:~:text=Prevalence%20of%20childhood%20and,the%20United%20States%2C%202011%E2%80%932012) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=Food%20and%20Beverage%20Sales,New%20Circana%20Research%20Reveals) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,my%20diet%20and%20nutrition)

## 3. Food Category Demand Shifts — Losers

The clearest near-term demand pressure from GLP-1 adoption is concentrated in categories built on appetite frequency, indulgence, and large portions. Across scanner-based retail work, shopper surveys, and industry analyses published through 2026, the most exposed categories are salty snacks, sweet baked goods, cookies, carbonated soft drinks, selected frozen and center-store staples, and several quick-service restaurant occasions where value has historically depended on combo meals, add-ons, and impulse consumption.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=The%20reduced%20appetite%20these,6.4%25%20year%20over%20year) [99](https://www.circana.com/post/transforming-food-beverage-choices-insights-from-glp-1-medication-users#:~:text=2025Circana%E2%80%99s%20latest%20insights%20reveal,Spending%20Related%20to%20GLP-1s) [107](https://www.occstrategy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/OCC-Understanding-the-GLP-1-Effect-on-Consumption-Behavior.pdf#:~:text=Percent%20Change%20in%20Grocery,drinks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E-6%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E%20.%20%3Ctr%3E%3Ctd%3EMeat%20snacks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E%2B1%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E)

The pattern is not a uniform collapse in food spending, but a selective erosion of low-nutrient, high-frequency categories as users report fewer eating occasions, smaller baskets, and more intentional choices. NIQ reported that shopping trips among GLP-1 users focused on weight loss declined 6.4% year over year, while Circana and other industry sources describe a reallocation away from indulgent center-store products and toward protein, hydration, and function-led items, implying that category losers are primarily those least aligned with satiety efficiency and nutrient density.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=The%20reduced%20appetite%20these,6.4%25%20year%20over%20year) [101](https://www.circana.com/post/the-rise-of-glp-1-medications-induces-evolving-baskets-not-shrinking-appetites-in-food-and-beverage#:~:text=Gain%20insight%20into%20GLP-1,with%20Circana%27s%20insight%20collection)

### 3.2. Impact on frozen foods, cheese, salad dressings, and center-store staples

The pressure extends beyond obvious indulgence categories into center-store staples that are tied to convenience meals, heavy meal assembly, and lower nutrient density. OCC Strategy’s 2025 category analysis identified declines in frozen foods, cheese, and salad dressings among GLP-1 households after adoption, indicating that the demand reset reaches into pantry and refrigerated staples, not just discretionary snacking.[107](https://www.occstrategy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/OCC-Understanding-the-GLP-1-Effect-on-Consumption-Behavior.pdf#:~:text=Percent%20Change%20in%20Grocery,drinks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E-6%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E%20.%20%3Ctr%3E%3Ctd%3EMeat%20snacks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E%2B1%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E)

- Frozen foods: The category is bifurcating rather than uniformly declining. Legacy frozen items associated with comfort food, pizza, and high-calorie convenience are under pressure, while manufacturers have responded by launching portion-controlled, protein-forward frozen meals specifically designed for GLP-1 users, implying that the category loss is concentrated in traditional formats rather than the entire frozen aisle.[110](https://www.foodexport-jp.org/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FoodLink_2026_3-4_en.pdf#:~:text=frozen%20products%20aren%E2%80%99t%20just,for%20quick%20weeknight%20dinners) [97](https://www.circana.com/industries/center-store-and-frozen-foods#:~:text=Notable%20growth%20includes%20Juice%2FDrink,and%20Lard%20up%2091.5%25)
- Cheese and salad dressings: These products are exposed because they often function as calorie-dense accompaniments rather than primary nutrition anchors. As users simplify meals and prioritize protein efficiency, add-on categories such as shredded cheese, creamy dressings, and rich toppings can lose units even when core meal consumption remains intact. This is an inference supported by reported category declines and by NIQ’s evidence of more intentional, lower-carbohydrate, smaller-meal behavior.[107](https://www.occstrategy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/OCC-Understanding-the-GLP-1-Effect-on-Consumption-Behavior.pdf#:~:text=Percent%20Change%20in%20Grocery,drinks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E-6%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E%20.%20%3Ctr%3E%3Ctd%3EMeat%20snacks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E%2B1%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=The%20reduced%20appetite%20these,6.4%25%20year%20over%20year)
- Center-store staples: Categories built around stock-up behavior, processed meal kits, and routine pantry replenishment are vulnerable when shopping trips fall. NIQ’s reported 6.4% year-over-year decline in shopping trips among GLP-1 users suggests fewer opportunities to add center-store impulse items, while Circana’s framing of “evolving baskets” indicates that basket composition is being rebuilt around function rather than shelf-stable abundance.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=The%20reduced%20appetite%20these,6.4%25%20year%20over%20year) [101](https://www.circana.com/post/the-rise-of-glp-1-medications-induces-evolving-baskets-not-shrinking-appetites-in-food-and-beverage#:~:text=Gain%20insight%20into%20GLP-1,with%20Circana%27s%20insight%20collection)

The market response has been adaptation rather than retreat. Frozen and center-store manufacturers are increasingly repositioning around portion control, protein density, digestive tolerance, and simpler ingredient decks, which itself is evidence that incumbent formats in these aisles are losing relevance with a growing GLP-1 user base.[110](https://www.foodexport-jp.org/cms/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FoodLink_2026_3-4_en.pdf#:~:text=frozen%20products%20aren%E2%80%99t%20just,for%20quick%20weeknight%20dinners) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones,place%20in%20the%20occasion)

### 3.3. Quick-service restaurant category declines (pizza, chicken, burgers, coffee/bakery)

Quick-service restaurants face a structurally different risk from retail because GLP-1 users do not need to eliminate restaurant visits for category economics to weaken. The more immediate effect is lower ticket composition, fewer sides and beverages, reduced dessert attachment, and weaker demand in categories built around large portions and craveability. This is consistent with restaurant industry adaptation in 2026 toward lighter, protein-forward, lower-carbohydrate menu options.[98](https://abcnews.com/US/weight-loss-drug-usage-grows-restaurants-offer-glp/story?id=135711648#:~:text=The%20menu%20features%20several,kick%20without%20the%20carbohydrates) [102](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/gas-prices-glp-1s-restaurant-lto-innovations/820445/#:~:text=Taco%20Bell%20will%20debut,specialty%20LTOs%20this%20year)

- Pizza: Pizza is among the most exposed QSR and delivery categories because it combines large portions, refined carbohydrates, cheese load, and group-ordering dynamics. Industry figures summarized in 2026 market commentary indicate pizza has seen one of the steepest household spending pullbacks among GLP-1 users, reflecting both lower personal intake and reduced appeal of leftovers and multi-slice occasions.[103](https://mealfan.com/glp-1-statistics-2026/#:~:text=Quick-service%20restaurant%20spending%20is,burgers%20down%206.4%20percent%29) [107](https://www.occstrategy.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/OCC-Understanding-the-GLP-1-Effect-on-Consumption-Behavior.pdf#:~:text=Percent%20Change%20in%20Grocery,drinks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E-6%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E%20.%20%3Ctr%3E%3Ctd%3EMeat%20snacks%3C%2Ftd%3E%3Ctd%3E%2B1%25%3C%2Ftd%3E%3C%2Ftr%3E)
- Chicken and burgers: These categories are not disappearing, but they are being reformatted. Operators are promoting bunless burgers, grilled chicken, smaller portions, and protein cups because traditional fried sandwiches, combo meals, and add-on fries are less aligned with GLP-1 eating patterns. That implies category pressure is greatest in legacy high-calorie builds rather than in lean-protein cores.[98](https://abcnews.com/US/weight-loss-drug-usage-grows-restaurants-offer-glp/story?id=135711648#:~:text=The%20menu%20features%20several,kick%20without%20the%20carbohydrates) [105](https://labs.meetnishi.com/#:~:text=Type%5BButton%3A%20Asian%5D%5BButton%3A%20Bowls%5D%5BButton%3A%20Breakfast%5D%5BButton%3A,Pizza%5D%5BButton%3A%20Salads%5D%5BButton%3A%20Sandwiches%5D%5BButton%3A%20Sit-down%5D)
- Coffee and bakery: Coffee-led chains are exposed through pastry attachment, sweetened beverages, and snack occasions rather than coffee alone. As appetite suppression reduces grazing and dessert demand, bakery mix and indulgent drink customization become more vulnerable than plain coffee or high-protein beverage formats.[103](https://mealfan.com/glp-1-statistics-2026/#:~:text=Quick-service%20restaurant%20spending%20is,burgers%20down%206.4%20percent%29) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones,place%20in%20the%20occasion)

The key investor takeaway is that GLP-1 pressure on QSR is category-specific and mix-driven, not simply traffic-driven. Even where restaurant frequency remains resilient, the economics of pizza, fried chicken, burgers with sides, and coffee-plus-bakery occasions can deteriorate if users trade down in portion size, skip beverages, or substitute toward protein-led items with lower attachment rates.[98](https://abcnews.com/US/weight-loss-drug-usage-grows-restaurants-offer-glp/story?id=135711648#:~:text=The%20menu%20features%20several,kick%20without%20the%20carbohydrates) [102](https://www.restaurantdive.com/news/gas-prices-glp-1s-restaurant-lto-innovations/820445/#:~:text=Taco%20Bell%20will%20debut,specialty%20LTOs%20this%20year) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=The%20reduced%20appetite%20these,6.4%25%20year%20over%20year)

## 4. Food Category Demand Shifts — Winners

The demand upside from GLP-1 adoption is concentrated in categories that deliver satiety, nutrient density, digestive tolerance, and health signaling per eating occasion. Across 2025 to 2026 scanner analyses, shopper panels, and industry surveys, the strongest beneficiaries are fresh produce, yogurt, nutrition bars, protein snacks, vitamins, supplements, and adjacent health-oriented products, indicating that GLP-1 is reallocating spend rather than simply shrinking food demand overall.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=wearable%20usage%20is%20associated,and%20fresh%20produce%20consumption) [99](https://www.circana.com/post/transforming-food-beverage-choices-insights-from-glp-1-medication-users#:~:text=2025Circana%E2%80%99s%20latest%20insights%20reveal,more%20functional%20snacking%20options) [129](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/industries/consumer%20packaged%20goods/our%20insights/state%20of%20food%20and%20beverage/stateoffoodbeveragethechoicescpgleaderscanmaketorenewgrowthfinal.pdf#:~:text=Source%3A%20Sylvia%20Hristakeva%20et,2025%2C%E2%80%9D%20Cornell%20SC%20Johnson)

This shift is consistent with the biological and behavioral profile of GLP-1 users already established in earlier sections, but the commercial significance here is category mix. Winning categories are those that help consumers eat less while feeling nutritionally covered, which supports premiumization in protein and wellness while improving the relative resilience of perimeter and function-led categories.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=adoption%20is%20uneven%20as,of%20~%244k%E2%80%93%245k%20per%20year) [122](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306919225002313#:~:text=GLP-1%20medications%20reduce%20price,for%20some%20protein%20goods) [101](https://www.circana.com/post/the-rise-of-glp-1-medications-induces-evolving-baskets-not-shrinking-appetites-in-food-and-beverage#:~:text=GLP-1%20households%20increasingly%20favor,and%20practice%20leader%2C%20Circana)

- Fresh produce: NIQ reported in 2026 that wearable usage is associated with increased water, protein, and fresh produce consumption, and it positioned GLP-1 users within a broader self-directed health consumer shift that is moving spend toward minimally processed, health-signaling foods. This matters because produce benefits not only from calorie reduction, but from substitution away from heavy center-store meal components.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=wearable%20usage%20is%20associated,and%20fresh%20produce%20consumption)
- Yogurt: NIQ’s 2026 intentional appetite analysis states that newer GLP-1 users over-index in nutritious categories including yogurt and fresh fruit, suggesting yogurt is functioning as a high-protein, portion-friendly, easy-to-tolerate format early in the treatment journey. Expo West 2026 coverage from NIQ also highlighted yogurt brands leaning into probiotic and prebiotic claims, reinforcing yogurt’s role at the intersection of protein and gut health.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=adoption%20is%20uneven%20as,of%20~%244k%E2%80%93%245k%20per%20year) [124](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/expo-west-2026-trends/#:~:text=This%20year%E2%80%99s%20show%20revealed,satisfaction%20and%20functional%20benefits)
- Nutrition bars and protein snacks: Circana’s 2025 update on GLP-1 households describes a noticeable shift toward snacks offering protein, hydration, energy boosts, and convenience, while NIQ’s 2026 health and wellness report says category convergence is accelerating at the intersection of supplements, snacks, and beverages. That combination favors bars, jerky, high-protein bites, and other compact formats that justify their place in fewer eating occasions.[99](https://www.circana.com/post/transforming-food-beverage-choices-insights-from-glp-1-medication-users#:~:text=2025Circana%E2%80%99s%20latest%20insights%20reveal,more%20functional%20snacking%20options) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=wearable%20usage%20is%20associated,and%20fresh%20produce%20consumption)

The analytical point is that these categories are not merely “healthy” in a generic sense. They are winning because they solve the GLP-1 consumption equation of lower appetite, higher selectivity, and greater demand for nutritional efficiency per bite, which gives perimeter fresh and functional snack formats a structural advantage over frequency-led indulgence categories.[104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=it%E2%80%99s%20challenging%20traditional%20consumption-based%20innovation%20research) [101](https://www.circana.com/post/the-rise-of-glp-1-medications-induces-evolving-baskets-not-shrinking-appetites-in-food-and-beverage#:~:text=GLP-1%20households%20increasingly%20favor,and%20practice%20leader%2C%20Circana)

### 4.2. Increased spending on vitamins, supplements, and health-oriented products

GLP-1 adoption is also redirecting spending into non-food wellness categories that consumers perceive as supporting metabolic health, nutrient adequacy, digestive comfort, and overall self-management. NIQ’s 2025 Consumer Outlook to 2026 explicitly identifies incrementality in CPG for fiber and protein, digestive health, supplements and vitamins, and notes that consumers are shifting spend away from food excess toward medications, lifestyle, and health-oriented categories.[123](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/report/2025/consumer-outlook-guide-to-2026/#:~:text=more%20consumers%20are%20planning,Fresh%20Meat%2C%20and%20Dairy)

- Supplements and vitamins: NIQ’s 2026 health and wellness analysis says winning categories are emerging where supplements, snacks, and beverages converge, indicating that consumers increasingly want function embedded into everyday consumption rather than separated into a purely pharmaceutical routine. This is especially relevant for GLP-1 users managing reduced intake and seeking reassurance around nutrient sufficiency.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=wearable%20usage%20is%20associated,and%20fresh%20produce%20consumption)
- Health-oriented basket expansion: Circana’s GLP-1 consumer behavior materials describe evolving baskets rather than shrinking appetites, with stronger orientation toward protein, hydration, energy support, and convenience. That framing implies that vitamins and wellness products are part of a broader basket redesign around intentional health maintenance, not an isolated add-on purchase.[125](https://www.circana.com/insights-hub/glp-1s-consumer-behavior#:~:text=The%206%20Most%20Surprising,Circana%E2%80%99s%20Latest%20GLP-1%20Research) [101](https://www.circana.com/post/the-rise-of-glp-1-medications-induces-evolving-baskets-not-shrinking-appetites-in-food-and-beverage#:~:text=GLP-1%20households%20increasingly%20favor,and%20practice%20leader%2C%20Circana)
- Market signaling: NIQ’s Expo West 2026 review described an expanded universe of metabolic-supporting supplements tailored to more health-attentive consumers, showing that manufacturers and retailers are already treating GLP-1-influenced demand as a product development and merchandising opportunity. The commercial implication is that supplement growth is being reinforced by both consumer pull and supply-side innovation.[124](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/expo-west-2026-trends/#:~:text=This%20year%E2%80%99s%20show%20revealed,satisfaction%20and%20functional%20benefits)

Purchasing behavior appears to be changing in two ways. First, consumers are reallocating dollars from discretionary calories toward products that signal control, adequacy, and wellness. Second, they are increasingly comfortable buying across category boundaries, such as protein-plus-gut-health or snack-plus-supplement formats, which expands the addressable market for health-oriented products beyond the traditional vitamin aisle.[123](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/report/2025/consumer-outlook-guide-to-2026/#:~:text=more%20consumers%20are%20planning,Fresh%20Meat%2C%20and%20Dairy) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=wearable%20usage%20is%20associated,and%20fresh%20produce%20consumption)

This matters for investors because supplements and adjacent wellness products can capture a disproportionate share of wallet even if total food volume falls. In a GLP-1 environment, categories that help consumers feel nutritionally optimized may benefit from both higher frequency of consideration and stronger premium acceptance than conventional packaged foods.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=adoption%20is%20uneven%20as,of%20~%244k%E2%80%93%245k%20per%20year) [123](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/report/2025/consumer-outlook-guide-to-2026/#:~:text=more%20consumers%20are%20planning,Fresh%20Meat%2C%20and%20Dairy)

### 4.3. Shifts in willingness to pay and price sensitivity for protein products

Protein is the clearest example of GLP-1-driven premium resilience. A 2025 Food Policy study found that GLP-1 use increases willingness to pay for most evaluated protein products and reduces price sensitivity for some protein goods, meaning users’ purchase volumes respond less to price changes than those of non-users.[122](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306919225002313#:~:text=GLP-1%20medications%20reduce%20price,for%20some%20protein%20goods) [127](https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/379045/files/McCluskey_Bina_Tonsor_Richards_GLP-1%20Manuscript_AgEcon.pdf#:~:text=2024%29.This%20study%20quantifies%20GLP-1%E2%80%99s,in%20retail%20and%20foodservice)

- Lower price sensitivity: The Food Policy findings imply that discounting is less effective as a demand lever for GLP-1 protein shoppers than for conventional shoppers. For manufacturers, that supports a strategy centered on value-added protein formats, quality cues, and functional differentiation rather than pure price competition.[122](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306919225002313#:~:text=GLP-1%20medications%20reduce%20price,for%20some%20protein%20goods)
- Higher willingness to pay: NIQ’s 2025 Consumer Outlook reported that more than half of consumers say they will pay a premium for fresh, clean, and healthier options, while NIQ’s 2026 intentional appetite analysis recommends sharper pack-price architecture and clearer value communication for GLP-1-influenced shoppers. Together, these sources suggest that protein products can sustain premium pricing when the benefit is explicit, credible, and easy to integrate into smaller eating occasions.[123](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/report/2025/consumer-outlook-guide-to-2026/#:~:text=more%20consumers%20are%20planning,Fresh%20Meat%2C%20and%20Dairy) [100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=adoption%20is%20uneven%20as,of%20~%244k%E2%80%93%245k%20per%20year)
- Buyer mix effect: The AgEconSearch version of the protein-demand study notes that lower own-price sensitivity implies GLP-1 users may represent a larger share of aggregate protein purchases as prices rise, because non-users cut back more sharply. That creates a subtle but important mix shift in which protein categories become increasingly dependent on a consumer segment that values satiety and function over lowest price.[127](https://ageconsearch.umn.edu/record/379045/files/McCluskey_Bina_Tonsor_Richards_GLP-1%20Manuscript_AgEcon.pdf#:~:text=2024%29.This%20study%20quantifies%20GLP-1%E2%80%99s,in%20retail%20and%20foodservice)

The broader implication is that GLP-1 is not only changing what consumers buy, but how they evaluate value. In protein, value is moving away from calories per dollar and toward satiety, nutrient density, convenience, and tolerance, which supports premium snacks, dairy protein, meat snacks, and fortified formats even in a generally price-conscious consumer environment.[99](https://www.circana.com/post/transforming-food-beverage-choices-insights-from-glp-1-medication-users#:~:text=2025Circana%E2%80%99s%20latest%20insights%20reveal,more%20functional%20snacking%20options) [124](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/expo-west-2026-trends/#:~:text=This%20year%E2%80%99s%20show%20revealed,satisfaction%20and%20functional%20benefits)

For category strategy, this means protein should be treated as a margin opportunity as much as a volume opportunity. Brands that can demonstrate clear functional payoff, portion appropriateness, and clean-label credibility are better positioned than brands relying on commodity protein claims alone.[122](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0306919225002313#:~:text=GLP-1%20medications%20reduce%20price,for%20some%20protein%20goods) [100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=adoption%20is%20uneven%20as,of%20~%244k%E2%80%93%245k%20per%20year)

## 5. Consumer Behavior and Lifestyle Transformation

GLP-1 adoption is increasingly associated with a broader lifestyle reset rather than a narrow reduction in food volume. Clinical evidence through 2026 shows meaningful reductions in energy intake under treatment, while commercial panel and survey data indicate that users often translate lower appetite into more intentional eating, fewer processed-food occasions, and higher spending on categories linked to health management, body change, and self-presentation.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=Methods%3A%20We%20searched%20six,dual%20GIP%2FGLP-1%20receptor%20agonist) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,in%202024%20and%202025%29)

The long-term significance for industry is that these changes appear to diffuse beyond the treated individual and into household routines, wardrobe needs, exercise behavior, and self-care purchases. At the same time, discontinuation remains a major fault line: available 2025 to 2026 reviews suggest that biological rebound after stopping therapy is common, so the durability of lifestyle transformation depends less on the drug alone than on whether new habits become embedded in household systems and consumer identity.[140](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/glp-1-consumer-behavior-shift#:~:text=People%20taking%20GLP-1s%20plan,to%20keep%20themselves%20fit) [153](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41574-026-01258-5#:~:text=data%20from%20the%20past,first%20year%20of%20therapy) [165](https://www.bmj.com/content/392/bmj-2025-085304)

### 5.2. Household spillover effect and behavioral persistence after discontinuation

Household spillover is now one of the most commercially important features of GLP-1 adoption. PwC reported that as of May 2026, about 21% of U.S. households included a current GLP-1 user, up from 9% in January 2025 based on Numerator analysis, while BCG found that non-users living with a GLP-1 user adopt roughly 30% to 50% of the user’s behavior changes, including eating fewer processed foods and less fast food and consuming more fruits and vegetables.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications) [140](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/glp-1-consumer-behavior-shift#:~:text=People%20taking%20GLP-1s%20plan,to%20keep%20themselves%20fit)

This spillover helps explain why household-level demand effects can exceed what individual prevalence alone would suggest. The 2025 consumer demand study by Hristakeva, Liaukonytė, and Feler estimated an average grocery spending decline of 5.3% in the six months after adoption, and Numerator-linked public slides reported an approximately 6% decline for households with at least one GLP-1 user, indicating that shared pantry, meal planning, and restaurant decisions amplify the effect.[158](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/suppl/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=We%20then%20assess%20the,these%20changes%20over%20time) [149](https://affi.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Numerator-Slides-AFFICON.pdf#:~:text=Utilizing%20a%20unique%20dataset,spending%20by%20nearly%209%25)

Persistence after discontinuation is more uncertain and should be treated cautiously. Reviews published in 2026 consistently conclude that stopping GLP-1 therapy is commonly followed by weight regain and deterioration in cardiometabolic markers, with the BMJ meta-analysis estimating average regain of 6.0 kg within the first year after stopping incretin mimetics and a projected return to baseline weight around 1.6 years after cessation.[165](https://www.bmj.com/content/392/bmj-2025-085304) [153](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41574-026-01258-5#:~:text=data%20from%20the%20past,first%20year%20of%20therapy)

- Biological persistence is weak: Current evidence suggests the pharmacologic appetite effect fades after discontinuation, so behavioral persistence cannot be assumed from treatment history alone.[153](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41574-026-01258-5#:~:text=data%20from%20the%20past,first%20year%20of%20therapy) [152](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41889156/#:~:text=Aims%3A%20This%20review%20aims,framework%20for%20post-treatment%20management)
- Behavioral persistence is conditional: Reviews on post-discontinuation maintenance emphasize that sustained outcomes depend on integrated lifestyle support, structured nutrition habits, and ongoing behavioral reinforcement rather than medication withdrawal by itself.[156](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13240724/#:~:text=the%20percentage%20of%20adults,uncertainty%20regarding%20long-term%20safety) [162](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12768930/#:~:text=a%206.9%20%25%20body,at%20week%2088%20%5B148%5D)
- Commercial implication: Even if some individual users discontinue, household routines learned during treatment may preserve part of the basket shift, especially where the treated user changed shared shopping lists, meal composition, and restaurant norms. This is an inference from the coexistence of strong household spillover evidence and weak biological persistence after cessation.[140](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/glp-1-consumer-behavior-shift#:~:text=People%20taking%20GLP-1s%20plan,to%20keep%20themselves%20fit) [165](https://www.bmj.com/content/392/bmj-2025-085304)

### 5.3. Spending reallocation toward fitness, apparel, and self-care

The consumer wallet effect increasingly extends beyond food. PwC reported in 2026 that 73% of current GLP-1 users reported a meaningful change in clothing size, 26% said they were spending more on clothing overall, and apparel spend was up 9.9% among GLP-1 users after 6 to 8 months on the drug.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications)

Fitness and health-management spending are also rising, although unevenly by user segment. PwC found that 36% of current users had increased cardio activity such as walking, running, cycling, or swimming, while BCG’s 2026 segmentation described a large “lifestyle optimizer” cohort, representing 41% of surveyed users, that showed the strongest spending increases in fitness memberships, wearables, and apparel.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications) [140](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/glp-1-consumer-behavior-shift#:~:text=People%20taking%20GLP-1s%20plan,to%20keep%20themselves%20fit)

Self-care categories are benefiting as body change and health attention spill into adjacent purchases. NIQ reported that GLP-1 weight-loss users spend 1.3 times more on health and beauty products than nonusers, including 97% more on fragrance, 58% more on sun care, 53% more on hair products, and 39% more on deodorants.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,in%202024%20and%202025%29)

- Apparel reactivation: Weight loss creates both functional replacement demand and identity-driven spending, with users buying smaller sizes, activewear, and transitional wardrobes as their bodies change.[146](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/business-model-reinvention/glp-1-trends-and-impact-on-business-models.html#:~:text=Health%20and%20wellness%20businesses,to%20newly%20emerging%20ones) [147](https://www.retaildive.com/news/retail-apparel-impact-glp-1-weight-loss-drugs/815662/#:~:text=Shoppers%20are%20buying%20apparel,lifestyle%20changes%2C%20Circana%20found)
- Fitness reallocation: Increased exercise is not universal, but a meaningful share of users appear to complement medication with cardio, wearables, and structured wellness routines, supporting adjacent demand in gyms, connected fitness, and sports apparel.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications) [140](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/glp-1-consumer-behavior-shift#:~:text=People%20taking%20GLP-1s%20plan,to%20keep%20themselves%20fit)
- Self-care premiumization: Beauty, grooming, and body-confidence categories are emerging as secondary beneficiaries of GLP-1 adoption, suggesting that the economic effect is partly a reallocation from calories toward appearance, maintenance, and health signaling.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,in%202024%20and%202025%29) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications)

For investors, this means GLP-1 exposure should be assessed across a wider consumer ecosystem than food alone. The strongest medium-term beneficiaries may include apparel, activewear, wearables, beauty, and wellness services that monetize sustained body change and health engagement, even if food volume growth slows.[140](https://www.bcg.com/publications/2026/glp-1-consumer-behavior-shift#:~:text=People%20taking%20GLP-1s%20plan,to%20keep%20themselves%20fit) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,in%202024%20and%202025%29) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=The%20data%20points%20to,shift%20with%20lasting%20implications)

## 6. Clinical and Retail Evidence Review

This section connects two evidence streams that are often discussed separately. Clinical studies measure how GLP-1 and dual incretin therapies change energy intake under standardized conditions, while retail and household panel studies observe how those physiological effects translate into grocery and foodservice spending in everyday life.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company) [189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Article%20first%20published%20online%3A%20December%2018%2C%202025)

The integration challenge is that these literatures use different units, time horizons, and populations. Clinical meta-analyses typically report kilojoule or kilocalorie reductions in controlled settings, whereas scanner studies report changes in dollars, trips, and category mix, making translation from intake suppression to category demand neither automatic nor one to one.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company) [190](https://www.numerator.com/resources/blog/consumer-impacts-glp-1/#:~:text=GLP-1%20households%20decreased%20food,than%20non-GLP-1%20households%20%28-0.5%25%29)

### 6.1. Meta-analytic findings on caloric intake reduction under standardized conditions

The strongest current synthesis is the 2026 systematic review and meta-analysis of quantitative dietary outcomes during GLP-1 or dual GIP GLP-1 therapy. It searched six databases through March 31, 2026 and found pooled reductions in energy intake of about 1,132 kJ per day in the core analysis, rising to about 1,421 kJ per day when an additional phase 1 trial was included, equivalent to roughly 270 to 340 kcal per day under study conditions.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company)

- Standardization strength: These estimates are valuable because they aggregate studies using structured dietary assessment and controlled treatment contexts, reducing noise from seasonality, household sharing, and self-directed diet changes.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company)
- Mechanistic consistency: The 2025 scoping review on nutrition strategies for semaglutide and tirzepatide concluded that the evidence base consistently points to reduced appetite, lower spontaneous intake, and the need for nutrition support to preserve protein adequacy and diet quality during treatment.[170](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41500509/#:~:text=%E2%80%9CSemaglutide%20and%20Cardiovascular%20Outcomes,-%20DOI%20-%20PubMed) [199](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42280393/)
- Boundary condition: Even this meta-analytic evidence should not be read as a direct forecast of category sales loss, because standardized meal tests and trial-linked dietary records capture biological response more cleanly than they capture substitution across foods, channels, and household members.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company) [172](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42440974/#:~:text=28%286%29%3A1050%E2%80%931061.%20-%20PMC%20-%20PubMed)

A useful nuance for investors is that the clinical literature is stronger on total intake suppression than on dietary quality improvement. The CRAVE study, published in 2026, reported reductions in energy intake and micronutrient consumption in a real-world cohort without structured nutrition support, but no improvement in diet quality or food cravings, reinforcing that lower calories do not automatically imply better habitual eating patterns.[172](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42440974/#:~:text=28%286%29%3A1050%E2%80%931061.%20-%20PMC%20-%20PubMed)

### 6.2. U.S. scanner data and household expenditure analysis

The most decision-useful U.S. retail evidence comes from linked household panel and transaction data rather than from self-reported food diaries. The Journal of Marketing Research paper by Hristakeva, Liaukonytė, and Feler uses survey responses on GLP-1 adoption linked to a representative U.S. household transaction panel from July 2022 to September 2024 and finds that households with at least one GLP-1 user reduce grocery spending by 5.3% within six months of adoption, with the decline reaching about 8.2% among higher-income households.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Article%20first%20published%20online%3A%20December%2018%2C%202025) [185](https://news.cornell.edu/stories/2025/12/ozempic-changing-foods-americans-buy#:~:text=The%20headline%20finding%20is,following%20adoption%2C%E2%80%9D%20Hristakeva%20said)

- Household level effect: The same study is important because it measures spending at the household level, which is the relevant unit for grocery demand, pantry stocking, and shared meal decisions rather than only the treated individual’s intake.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Article%20first%20published%20online%3A%20December%2018%2C%202025)
- Channel extension: Public summaries of the research report an approximately 8% to 8.6% decline in spending at fast-food chains, coffee shops, and limited-service restaurants, indicating that the effect is not confined to at-home grocery baskets.[188](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12992-026-01217-9#:~:text=a%20nationally%20representative%20US,spending%20by%208.2%25%20%5B8%5D) [196](https://producebusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PB_April_2025_io.pdf#:~:text=an%20estimated%208.3%25%20of,rather%20than%20diabetes%20management)
- Category mix insight: Numerator’s 2026 public analysis shows GLP-1 households decreased food spending by 1.6% in the first nine months of the year versus the prior year, compared with a 0.5% decline for non-GLP-1 households, while packaged sweet bakery goods, chips, packaged cookies, and cold cuts underperformed in GLP-1 households and items such as sugar substitutes, canned fruit, and dried fruit performed relatively better.[190](https://www.numerator.com/resources/blog/consumer-impacts-glp-1/#:~:text=GLP-1%20households%20decreased%20food,than%20non-GLP-1%20households%20%28-0.5%25%29)
- External validation: McKinsey’s 2026 food and beverage report reproduces the broad pattern from the Cornell linked-panel work, showing average spending declines in indulgence-heavy categories and relative resilience or gains in produce, yogurt, and nutrition bars after adoption.[129](https://www.mckinsey.com/~/media/mckinsey/industries/consumer%20packaged%20goods/our%20insights/state%20of%20food%20and%20beverage/stateoffoodbeveragethechoicescpgleaderscanmaketorenewgrowthfinal.pdf#:~:text=Source%3A%20Sylvia%20Hristakeva%20et,paper%2C%20Dec%2027%2C%202024)

For industry interpretation, scanner evidence is more commercially actionable than clinical intake data because it captures what households actually buy, not only what participants consume in a study protocol. Its limitation is that spending changes combine volume, price mix, channel substitution, and household spillover, so a lower grocery bill cannot be mapped cleanly into a single calorie reduction estimate.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Article%20first%20published%20online%3A%20December%2018%2C%202025) [190](https://www.numerator.com/resources/blog/consumer-impacts-glp-1/#:~:text=GLP-1%20households%20decreased%20food,than%20non-GLP-1%20households%20%28-0.5%25%29)

### 6.3. Gaps between controlled study findings and real-world habitual intake data

The central gap is that controlled studies show a fairly robust reduction in energy intake, but real-world evidence shows a noisier translation into diet quality, nutrient adequacy, and persistent household behavior. In other words, the pharmacologic signal is strong, but the behavioral and commercial expression of that signal depends on income, household composition, nutrition support, symptom burden, and whether consumers substitute toward better foods or simply fewer foods.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company) [172](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42440974/#:~:text=28%286%29%3A1050%E2%80%931061.%20-%20PMC%20-%20PubMed)

- Measurement mismatch: Clinical studies usually report kilojoules, kilocalories, appetite scores, or ad libitum meal intake, whereas retail studies report dollars, units, trips, and category shares. This creates an integration problem because a 300 kcal daily reduction does not specify whether the lost demand comes from snacks, beverages, restaurant add-ons, or smaller portions of core meals.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company) [189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Article%20first%20published%20online%3A%20December%2018%2C%202025)
- Diet quality ambiguity: The CRAVE study found reduced energy intake and micronutrient consumption without improvement in diet quality or food cravings in a real-world setting without structured nutrition support, suggesting that lower intake alone may not produce the healthier basket implied by some market narratives.[172](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42440974/#:~:text=28%286%29%3A1050%E2%80%931061.%20-%20PMC%20-%20PubMed)
- Heterogeneity problem: The 2025 scoping review of nutrition strategies concluded that the evidence base remains fragmented across semaglutide and tirzepatide studies, with inconsistent dietary assessment methods and limited integration of formal nutrition interventions, which weakens comparability across trials and real-world cohorts.[170](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41500509/#:~:text=%E2%80%9CSemaglutide%20and%20Cardiovascular%20Outcomes,-%20DOI%20-%20PubMed)
- Persistence uncertainty: Retail studies suggest spending reductions can persist through the first year of use, but public summaries also indicate grocery spending rebounds after discontinuation, implying that part of the demand effect is medication-contingent rather than permanently habit-forming.[196](https://producebusiness.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/PB_April_2025_io.pdf#:~:text=an%20estimated%208.3%25%20of,rather%20than%20diabetes%20management) [188](https://doi.org/10.1186/s12992-026-01217-9#:~:text=a%20nationally%20representative%20US,spending%20by%208.2%25%20%5B8%5D)

For investors and operators, the practical conclusion is that clinical efficacy should be treated as a necessary but insufficient predictor of food demand change. The most reliable bridge between the two literatures is not a direct calorie to dollar conversion, but a framework that combines standardized intake suppression, household spillover, category substitution, and discontinuation risk when estimating long-run exposure by food category and channel.[142](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42508090/#:~:text=The%20scope%20of%20disclosure,Eli%20Lilly%20and%20Company) [189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Article%20first%20published%20online%3A%20December%2018%2C%202025) [199](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42280393/)

## 7. Food and Beverage Industry Response

Food and beverage companies are no longer treating GLP-1 adoption as a peripheral wellness trend. By 2026, the industry response has shifted from defensive monitoring to active portfolio redesign, with manufacturers and restaurants emphasizing protein, fiber, hydration, digestive tolerance, smaller portions, and clearer nutrition signaling in order to stay relevant as eating occasions become fewer and more intentional.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,in%202024%20and%202025%29) [100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=NIQ%20data%20suggests%20that,early%20in%20the%20journey) [202](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/propel-launches-clear-protein-a-new-take-functional-hydration-with-20g-protein-fiber-electrolytes#:~:text=The%20brand%E2%80%99s%20broader%20product,USDA%20Food%20Data%20Central)

The strategic pattern is consistent across CPG and foodservice. Brands are reformulating around nutrient density per calorie, experimenting with explicit GLP-1 support claims or badges, and redesigning menus to reduce friction for consumers who want smaller, higher-protein, lower-sugar, and easier-to-tolerate options without sacrificing convenience or taste.[218](https://www.smoothieking.com/news/smoothie-king-launches-glp-1-support-menu-to-support-health-and-fitness-journeys-to-medication-users/#:~:text=2024%29%20%E2%80%93%20Smoothie%20King%2C,or%20weight%20management%20goals) [206](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/02/13/glp-1-restaurants#:~:text=co-founder%20of%20the%20Cuba,people%20on%20GLP-1%20medications) [210](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/07/restaurant-menu-high-protein-dunkin-subway-chipotle#:~:text=It%20builds%20on%20the,dedicated%20GLP-1%20Support%20menu)

### 7.1. Product reformulation and innovation strategies (protein-forward, nutrient-dense formats)

Manufacturers are increasingly reformulating for what can be called nutritional efficiency. NIQ reported in 2026 that GLP-1 users are disproportionately focused on protein, fiber, probiotics, nutrient-rich foods, and simpler ingredients, while their reduced appetite is driving demand for smaller meals, snackable formats, and functional beverages with added nutrients or protein.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Despite%20representing%20just%204.5%25,in%202024%20and%202025%29) [100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=NIQ%20data%20suggests%20that,early%20in%20the%20journey)

- Reformulation logic: The design brief is shifting from indulgence and volume toward satiety, muscle preservation, digestive support, and hydration, because fewer eating occasions raise the nutritional stakes of each serving.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=NIQ%20data%20suggests%20that,early%20in%20the%20journey)
- Beverage innovation: PepsiCo launched Propel Clear Protein in May 2026 with 20g of whey protein, 3g of fiber, electrolytes, zero sugar, and 90 calories per packet, explicitly stating that the product was developed with registered dietitians and with GLP-1 users in mind.[202](https://www.pepsico.com/newsroom/press-releases/2026/propel-launches-clear-protein-a-new-take-functional-hydration-with-20g-protein-fiber-electrolytes#:~:text=The%20brand%E2%80%99s%20broader%20product,USDA%20Food%20Data%20Central)
- Meal and smoothie innovation: Smoothie King launched a GLP-1 Support Menu in October 2024 and by 2026 continued merchandising smoothies built around protein, fiber, and 0g added sugar, while Daily Harvest marketed GLP-1 smoothies with up to 20g protein and up to 9g fiber.[218](https://www.smoothieking.com/news/smoothie-king-launches-glp-1-support-menu-to-support-health-and-fitness-journeys-to-medication-users/#:~:text=2024%29%20%E2%80%93%20Smoothie%20King%2C,or%20weight%20management%20goals) [217](https://www.smoothieking.com/menu/smoothies/glp-1/#:~:text=Menu%2FSmoothies%2FGLP-1%20%23%20GLP-1%20Recipes,and%200g%20added%20sugar) [222](https://daily-harvest.com/collections/glp1-smoothies#:~:text=except%20our%20Protein%20Powder,healthy%20food%20on%20hand)

### 7.2. GLP-1 Friendly labeling trends and brand positioning

Labeling has moved from implicit wellness cues to explicit GLP-1 relevance. By 2025 and 2026, brands were increasingly using terms such as GLP-1 friendly, GLP-1 support, or companion positioning to reduce search costs for consumers seeking products that fit smaller appetites, higher protein targets, and lower added sugar or calorie loads.[235](https://apnews.com/article/5bb5dfaae3778b1968c1793326e8ba73#:~:text=Conagra%20Brands%20added%20a,like%20Factor%27s%20%22GLP-1%20Balance.%22) [217](https://www.smoothieking.com/menu/smoothies/glp-1/#:~:text=Menu%2FSmoothies%2FGLP-1%20%23%20GLP-1%20Recipes,and%200g%20added%20sugar) [223](https://daily-harvest.com/collections/dietary-needs#:~:text=Our%20Plant-based%20Whole30%C2%AE%20compatible,journey%20easy-breezy%20%28and%20delicious%29)

- Explicit menu and pack language: Smoothie King uses a dedicated GLP-1 label and states that its recipes are designed to support a GLP-1 routine with protein, fiber, and 0g added sugar.[217](https://www.smoothieking.com/menu/smoothies/glp-1/#:~:text=Menu%2FSmoothies%2FGLP-1%20%23%20GLP-1%20Recipes,and%200g%20added%20sugar)
- Frozen meal badging: Industry documentation in 2025 described Conagra’s Healthy Choice On Track badge as a first-of-its-kind initiative to help shoppers identify meals marketed as GLP-1 friendly, centered on protein, calorie control, and fiber.[232](https://contentlibrary.ardentmills.com/m/235bf11eddb30c0f/original/the-kernel-july-2025.pdf#:~:text=Conagra%20created%20a%20first-of-its-kind,August%205%2C%202024%2C%20https%3A%2F%2Fhealth.clevelandclinic.org%2Fozempic-foods-to-avoid) [235](https://apnews.com/article/5bb5dfaae3778b1968c1793326e8ba73#:~:text=Conagra%20Brands%20added%20a,like%20Factor%27s%20%22GLP-1%20Balance.%22)
- Direct-to-consumer positioning: Daily Harvest created GLP-1-specific merchandising pages and frames its products around high protein, fiber, and fast preparation, showing how digital-first brands can target this segment without waiting for formal retail shelf resets.[222](https://daily-harvest.com/collections/glp1-smoothies#:~:text=except%20our%20Protein%20Powder,healthy%20food%20on%20hand) [223](https://daily-harvest.com/collections/dietary-needs#:~:text=Our%20Plant-based%20Whole30%C2%AE%20compatible,journey%20easy-breezy%20%28and%20delicious%29)

A key strategic nuance is that the label itself is doing two jobs. It signals nutritional suitability to current users, but it also acts as a broader shorthand for high-protein, lower-sugar, portion-conscious eating that appeals to non-users pursuing the same outcomes. This is consistent with NIQ’s 2026 argument that brands should invest in credible health positioning and make choices easier without compromising taste.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=NIQ%20data%20suggests%20that,early%20in%20the%20journey)

The risk is that GLP-1-friendly remains a marketing convention rather than a regulated standard. As of 2026, widely cited reporting noted that brands were using the term across frozen meals, smoothies, and meal kits, but there is no single formal nutrient threshold attached to the claim, which means credibility will increasingly depend on transparent macros, dietitian involvement, and consistency between claim and formulation.[235](https://apnews.com/article/5bb5dfaae3778b1968c1793326e8ba73#:~:text=Conagra%20Brands%20added%20a,like%20Factor%27s%20%22GLP-1%20Balance.%22) [221](https://www.smoothieking.com/nutrition/#:~:text=If%20you%E2%80%99re%20currently%20on,specifically%20for%20GLP-1%20support)

### 7.3. Restaurant adaptation: smaller portions, split-plate options, and menu redesign

Restaurant adaptation is moving faster in menu architecture than in overt branding. The most visible changes in 2026 are smaller portions, lighter builds, higher-protein options, lower-carbohydrate formats, and price points designed to preserve accessibility even when ticket sizes fall.[206](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/02/13/glp-1-restaurants#:~:text=co-founder%20of%20the%20Cuba,people%20on%20GLP-1%20medications) [210](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/07/restaurant-menu-high-protein-dunkin-subway-chipotle#:~:text=It%20builds%20on%20the,dedicated%20GLP-1%20Support%20menu)

- Portion redesign: Cuba Libre introduced a menu developed with a physician that features smaller portions, high protein, high fiber, moderate to low fat, moderate to low calories, and lower carbohydrate loads, then extended the concept into a broader lighter portions, lighter prices section on core menus.[206](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/02/13/glp-1-restaurants#:~:text=co-founder%20of%20the%20Cuba,people%20on%20GLP-1%20medications)
- Chain menu engineering: Axios reported in January 2026 that Dunkin’ added Protein Milk and Protein Refreshers, Subway launched Protein Pockets with more than 20g of protein at $3.99, Blaze Pizza introduced a cauliflower-crust Protein-zza marketed as GLP-1-friendly, and Smoothie King expanded bundled protein offerings.[210](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/07/restaurant-menu-high-protein-dunkin-subway-chipotle#:~:text=It%20builds%20on%20the,dedicated%20GLP-1%20Support%20menu)
- Consumer-facing rationale: Restaurant operators are responding not only to lower appetite, but to the need for diners to avoid wasting food, hit protein targets, and dine socially without being forced into oversized entrées or combo structures.[206](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/02/13/glp-1-restaurants#:~:text=co-founder%20of%20the%20Cuba,people%20on%20GLP-1%20medications) [207](https://www.nbcconnecticut.com/news/national-international/glp-1s-protein-chain-restaurant-menus/3689452/#:~:text=As%20GLP-1%20medications%20normalize,said%20in%20a%20statement)

The split-plate and smaller-entrée trend should be read as a margin-management issue as much as a nutrition issue. If GLP-1 users order fewer sides, desserts, and beverages, restaurants need menu designs that protect contribution dollars through premium protein, modular add-ons, and right-sized pricing rather than relying on legacy large-portion economics. This is an inference supported by the shift toward protein-led value menus and lighter-price sections across chains and independents.[210](https://www.axios.com/2026/01/07/restaurant-menu-high-protein-dunkin-subway-chipotle#:~:text=It%20builds%20on%20the,dedicated%20GLP-1%20Support%20menu) [206](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/02/13/glp-1-restaurants#:~:text=co-founder%20of%20the%20Cuba,people%20on%20GLP-1%20medications)

The likely next phase is normalization rather than segregation. Rather than every operator creating a dedicated GLP-1 menu, the more scalable model appears to be embedding smaller portions, customizable protein-forward builds, and transparent nutrition into mainstream menus so that GLP-1 users can order naturally alongside other diners.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=NIQ%20data%20suggests%20that,early%20in%20the%20journey) [206](https://www.wbur.org/hereandnow/2026/02/13/glp-1-restaurants#:~:text=co-founder%20of%20the%20Cuba,people%20on%20GLP-1%20medications)

## 8. Long-Term Market and Economic Projections

By 2026, the GLP-1 discussion in food and beverage has moved from category anecdotes to explicit long-range market modeling. The most cited forward views now frame GLP-1 adoption as a measurable earnings and mix issue for packaged food, grocery, and foodservice through 2030, with the largest downside concentrated in calorie-dense, frequency-led categories and the largest resilience in protein, produce, and function-led formats.[246](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/current-events/obesity-drugs#:~:text=Morgan%20Global%20Research%20forecasts,and%20Agribusiness%20at%20J.P) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones%20that%20redesign%20relevance)

The central analytical question is no longer whether GLP-1s affect food demand, but how durable and economically material that effect becomes as household penetration rises. Current evidence suggests that the long-term impact is best understood as a structural reallocation of demand and margin pools, moderated by discontinuation, reimbursement, and macroeconomic conditions rather than determined by any single one of them.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=Shifting%20size%20distributions%20can,and%20size%20curve%20assumptions) [75](https://www.circana.com/post/chain-reactions-the-impact-of-glp-1-usage-on-non-food-categories#:~:text=Taking%20it%20personally%3A%20Circana%E2%80%99s,usage%20compared%20to%20non-users) [100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=Image%3A%20Inline%20image%201,April%202026%20%7C%20Q10)

### 8.2. Circana projection: GLP-1 users as 35% of all U.S. food/beverage unit sales by 2030

Circana’s 2030 projection is one of the most commercially important demand-share forecasts in the market. Circana states that households using GLP-1 medications are projected to represent 35% of all U.S. food and beverage units sold by 2030, and related Circana materials indicate these households already account for a much larger share of spending influence than their raw user count would suggest.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [75](https://www.circana.com/post/chain-reactions-the-impact-of-glp-1-usage-on-non-food-categories#:~:text=Taking%20it%20personally%3A%20Circana%E2%80%99s,usage%20compared%20to%20non-users)

- Why the share can exceed prevalence: Circana’s public materials and video summary indicate GLP-1 households were already about 23% of U.S. households when the forecast was published, while PwC reported that 21% of U.S. households included a current GLP-1 user as of May 2026. The projected 35% sales share therefore reflects both continued penetration growth and above-average commercial weight per household, not merely a one-for-one mapping from user prevalence to unit share.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [79](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NBxKQsMuc-U#:~:text=Key%20insights%20include%20increased,sustained%20health-conscious%20behaviors%20post-medication) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=Shifting%20size%20distributions%20can,and%20size%20curve%20assumptions)
- Empirical backing: Circana says GLP-1 users reduce overall retail food and beverage purchases but still outspend non-users, which is consistent with a household base that buys fewer units in some categories while maintaining higher total wallet influence. NIQ provides a parallel signal, reporting that GLP-1 users represented 4.5% of households in one panel cut yet drove 43% of sales growth for products with muscle health claims across consecutive 52-week periods in 2024 and 2025.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=According%20to%20the%20Global,growth%20rate%20of%207.6%25)
- Sales pattern implication: A 35% unit-share influence by 2030 does not imply a 35% collapse in food demand. It implies that more than one-third of unit movement could be shaped by households that shop with smaller appetites, higher protein orientation, lower tolerance for excess calories, and stronger preference for functional or portion-appropriate products.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones%20that%20redesign%20relevance)
- Persistence effect: Circana explicitly notes a sustained market impact as consumers move in and out of active medication use but retain some of the health-conscious behaviors adopted during treatment. That point is important because it suggests the 2030 sales-share forecast is partly behavioral and household-systemic, not purely dependent on uninterrupted medication adherence.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted)

The strategic reading of Circana’s forecast is that food companies should plan for a market where GLP-1-influenced households become a core demand-setting segment rather than a niche. Once a segment shapes roughly one-third of unit sales, assortment, pack architecture, innovation pipelines, and promotional strategy all need to be calibrated to its preferences even for products still bought by non-users.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=Image%3A%20Inline%20image%201,April%202026%20%7C%20Q10)

### 8.3. Structural shift vs. cyclical trend debate

The evidence through August 25, 2026 supports treating GLP-1 effects as primarily structural, though not linear. The structural case rests on three factors: rising household penetration, a very large remaining eligible population, and product, menu, and merchandising changes already being embedded by manufacturers and restaurants rather than treated as temporary promotions.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=Shifting%20size%20distributions%20can,and%20size%20curve%20assumptions) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones%20that%20redesign%20relevance)

- Structural argument: NIQ’s 2026 framing is explicit that the issue is not just which categories are at risk, but how deliberately consumers choose food when appetite can no longer be assumed. PwC similarly argues that the data point to a consumer behavior shift with lasting implications, while Circana describes sustained effects even as consumers cycle on and off medication.[104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones%20that%20redesign%20relevance) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=Shifting%20size%20distributions%20can,and%20size%20curve%20assumptions) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted)
- Cyclical counterargument: Some of the observed demand pressure is still sensitive to reimbursement, drug pricing, supply availability, and discontinuation. If coverage stalls or persistence rates disappoint, category impacts could moderate or become more concentrated in affluent households, making the path uneven even if the direction remains intact.[246](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/current-events/obesity-drugs#:~:text=Morgan%20Global%20Research%20forecasts,and%20Agribusiness%20at%20J.P) [247](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/services/consulting/strategy/glp-1-trends-and-impact-on-business-models.html#:~:text=%E2%80%9CNegative%E2%80%9D%20indicates%20a%20response,1%2C%202%2C%20or%203)
- Best synthesis: The most defensible interpretation is structural trend with cyclical amplitude. In other words, the underlying market is being rewired toward fewer, more intentional, more nutrient-efficient eating occasions, but the speed and breadth of that rewiring will fluctuate with payer policy, macro pressure, and treatment persistence.[100](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/designing-for-an-intentional-appetite-winning-in-an-era-of-intentional-consumption/#:~:text=Image%3A%20Inline%20image%201,April%202026%20%7C%20Q10) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones%20that%20redesign%20relevance) [249](https://www.pwc.com.au/retail-consumer-markets/a-new-disruptor-in-the-food-aisle.html#:~:text=This%20isn%27t%20a%20fad,it%27s%20a%20platform%20shift)
- Industry consequence: Structural shifts reward redesign, while cyclical shifts reward waiting. The current evidence favors redesign, because companies are already seeing enough household penetration and enough category divergence to justify permanent changes in pack size, protein density, menu architecture, and innovation priorities.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=they%E2%80%99ll%20be%20the%20ones%20that%20redesign%20relevance)

For long-term planning, the practical conclusion is that GLP-1 should be modeled like a secular demand modifier rather than a temporary shock. The exact revenue path will remain cyclical, but the direction of travel toward lower-calorie, higher-protein, portion-conscious, and function-led consumption now appears durable enough to influence 2030 portfolio strategy and valuation work.[246](https://www.jpmorgan.com/insights/global-research/current-events/obesity-drugs#:~:text=Morgan%20Global%20Research%20forecasts,and%20Agribusiness%20at%20J.P) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=This%20indicates%20a%20sustained,health-conscious%20behaviors%20they%20adopted) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=Shifting%20size%20distributions%20can,and%20size%20curve%20assumptions)

## 9. Strategic Implications for Stakeholders

For stakeholders across food manufacturing, retail, foodservice, and capital markets, the strategic issue is no longer whether GLP-1 adoption affects demand, but how quickly firms can reconfigure portfolios, pricing, merchandising, and governance around a consumer base that is eating less often and demanding more nutritional utility per occasion. The evidence reviewed earlier suggests that the most exposed businesses are those dependent on impulse calories, attachment purchases, and large portions, while the most advantaged are those able to convert reduced appetite into higher-value, protein-forward, portion-appropriate, and function-led offerings.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=clients%20can%20take%20immediate,fast-paced%2C%20and%20ever-changing%20economy) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=it%E2%80%99s%20challenging%20traditional%20consumption-based%20innovation%20research) [255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,to%20maximize%20long-term%20success)

The practical implication is that GLP-1 should be treated as a cross-functional planning variable spanning innovation, category management, investor communication, and regulatory affairs. Companies that respond only with marketing language risk credibility loss, while those that align product design, nutrition substantiation, and portfolio capital allocation with emerging clinical guidance are better positioned to defend share and capture premium growth.[256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036071/#:~:text=Copyright%20%C2%A9%202026%20European,Clinical%20Nutrition%20and%20Metabolism) [257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2025.100228#:~:text=A%20flowchart%20summarizing%20key,Table%206%2C%20Table%207) [258](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-intends-take-action-against-non-fda-approved-glp-1-drugs#:~:text=Food%20and%20Drug%20Administration,alternatives%20to%20FDA-approved%20drugs)

### 9.1. Implications for food manufacturers, retailers, and restaurant chains

Manufacturers, retailers, and restaurant chains face the same underlying demand shift but different operational exposures. Manufacturers are most exposed through portfolio mix and innovation productivity, retailers through assortment and shelf economics, and restaurant chains through ticket architecture and attachment-rate erosion as GLP-1 users order fewer sides, desserts, and caloric beverages.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=clients%20can%20take%20immediate,fast-paced%2C%20and%20ever-changing%20economy) [104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=it%E2%80%99s%20challenging%20traditional%20consumption-based%20innovation%20research)

- Food manufacturers: The priority is to re-rank innovation pipelines around nutritional efficiency, especially protein density, fiber, hydration, digestive tolerance, and portion control. Joint clinical guidance from ACLM, ASN, OMA, and The Obesity Society emphasizes adequate protein, micronutrient sufficiency, management of gastrointestinal side effects, and minimally processed nutrient-dense diets, which supports reformulation toward smaller, higher-function servings rather than simply lower-calorie line extensions.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,to%20maximize%20long-term%20success) [256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036071/#:~:text=Copyright%20%C2%A9%202026%20European,Clinical%20Nutrition%20and%20Metabolism)
- Retailers: The key response is to redesign category adjacency, search, and merchandising around mission-based baskets. NIQ argues that fewer eating occasions raise expectations for each purchase, implying stronger returns from clustering protein snacks, yogurt, hydration, supplements, and fresh convenience items rather than relying on legacy center-store traffic patterns.[104](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/education/2026/fewer-occasions-higher-expectations-glp1/#:~:text=it%E2%80%99s%20challenging%20traditional%20consumption-based%20innovation%20research) [124](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/expo-west-2026-trends/#:~:text=This%20dovetails%20with%20broader,reduction%20and%20reformulation%20solutions)
- Restaurant chains: Operators should assume mix pressure before traffic pressure. Clinical and endocrine guidance recommending smaller portions, slower eating, lower-fat choices, hydration, and adequate protein implies that menu engineering should emphasize right-sized entrées, modular protein add-ons, and lower-waste formats rather than defending legacy combo structures.[259](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S1530891X25009772#:~:text=protein%20restriction%20%28%3C0.8%20g%2Fkg%29,not%20treated%20with%20dialysis) [257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2025.100228#:~:text=A%20flowchart%20summarizing%20key,Table%206%2C%20Table%207)
- Shared strategic response: All three stakeholder groups should build GLP-1 segmentation into demand forecasting, pack-price architecture, and shopper analytics. Circana’s projection that GLP-1 user households could represent 35% of U.S. food and beverage sales by 2030 means this segment is large enough to influence mainstream assortment decisions, not just niche wellness programs.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=clients%20can%20take%20immediate,fast-paced%2C%20and%20ever-changing%20economy)
- Execution risk: The main mistake is superficial adaptation. Products marketed to GLP-1 users without credible macro balance, satiety value, or tolerance benefits may underperform as consumers and clinicians become more discerning about what actually supports therapy adherence and lean-mass preservation.[256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036071/#:~:text=Copyright%20%C2%A9%202026%20European,Clinical%20Nutrition%20and%20Metabolism) [267](https://www.obesity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Joint_Nutritional-Priorities-to-Support-GLP-1-Therapy-for-Obesity.pdf#:~:text=389%283%29%3A222%E2%80%93233.%20doi%3A10.1056%2FNEJMoa2307563.%20.%20%E2%80%9CGLP-1s,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D)

### 9.3. Policy and nutritional labeling considerations

Policy and labeling are becoming strategic issues because market adoption has outpaced formal standards for what qualifies as GLP-1-friendly food. As of August 25, 2026, clinical organizations have issued increasingly specific nutrition guidance for patients on GLP-1 therapy, but there is still no single regulated nutrient profile or front-of-pack standard governing GLP-1-related food claims, creating both whitespace and compliance risk.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,to%20maximize%20long-term%20success) [256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036071/#:~:text=Copyright%20%C2%A9%202026%20European,Clinical%20Nutrition%20and%20Metabolism)

- Labeling gap: Current expert guidance emphasizes protein adequacy, micronutrient monitoring, hydration, fiber, lean-mass preservation, and management of gastrointestinal side effects, but these priorities do not yet translate into a harmonized consumer label. That gap increases the risk of inconsistent claims across frozen meals, beverages, supplements, and restaurant menus.[257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2025.100228#:~:text=A%20flowchart%20summarizing%20key,Table%206%2C%20Table%207) [256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036071/#:~:text=Copyright%20%C2%A9%202026%20European,Clinical%20Nutrition%20and%20Metabolism)
- Regulatory risk: FDA’s April 2025 announcement that it intends to act against mass-marketed non-FDA-approved GLP-1 drugs is not a food-labeling rule, but it signals a tougher enforcement environment around GLP-1-adjacent commercialization and consumer confusion. Companies using GLP-1 language in food marketing should therefore expect greater scrutiny of implied efficacy, medical adjacency, and substantiation.[258](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-intends-take-action-against-non-fda-approved-glp-1-drugs#:~:text=Food%20and%20Drug%20Administration,alternatives%20to%20FDA-approved%20drugs)
- Policy opportunity: Industry groups and regulators could reduce confusion by developing voluntary guardrails for GLP-1-related claims, such as minimum protein thresholds, added-sugar limits, calorie density ranges, and disclosure of fiber, hydration, or micronutrient support. This is an inference grounded in the specificity of emerging clinical guidance and the current absence of a standardized consumer-facing framework.[267](https://www.obesity.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/Joint_Nutritional-Priorities-to-Support-GLP-1-Therapy-for-Obesity.pdf#:~:text=389%283%29%3A222%E2%80%93233.%20doi%3A10.1056%2FNEJMoa2307563.%20.%20%E2%80%9CGLP-1s,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [257](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.obpill.2025.100228#:~:text=A%20flowchart%20summarizing%20key,Table%206%2C%20Table%207)
- Nutritional labeling implication: More prominent display of protein grams, fiber content, micronutrient contribution, and portion suitability is likely to become more commercially relevant than broad low-calorie messaging alone. The 2026 medical nutrition review argues for protein intakes of at least 1.2 g/kg, structured micronutrient monitoring, and lean-mass preservation, which supports labels that help consumers assess adequacy rather than just restriction.[256](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42036071/#:~:text=Copyright%20%C2%A9%202026%20European,Clinical%20Nutrition%20and%20Metabolism)
- Strategic response: Companies should align claims with dietitian-reviewed criteria and maintain documentation linking product positioning to recognized guidance. That approach can improve credibility with retailers, reduce legal risk, and create a stronger bridge between consumer marketing and healthcare-adjacent nutrition expectations.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,to%20maximize%20long-term%20success) [263](https://www.eatrightpro.org/obesity-medication#:~:text=The%20Academy%20of%20Nutrition,and%20supporting%20long-term%20success)

## 10. Conclusion and Key Takeaways

The evidence reviewed across clinical studies, household transaction panels, retail scanner datasets, and industry forecasts supports a clear conclusion: GLP-1 drugs are no longer a niche healthcare variable but a material demand-shaping force for food, beverage, and foodservice markets through August 25, 2026. The most important implication is not uniform food deflation, but a re-ranking of categories around nutritional efficiency, with calorie-dense impulse products losing relevance and protein, produce, yogurt, hydration, and supplement-adjacent formats gaining strategic importance.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Using%20a%20combination%20of,8.2%25%20among%20higher-income%20households) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value)

The data also suggests that permanence should be understood as partial but meaningful. Pharmacologic effects are contingent on continued use, yet household spillovers, product reformulation, retailer assortment changes, and the embedding of protein-forward, portion-conscious habits indicate that at least part of the demand shift is likely to persist even as individual users cycle on and off therapy.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=What%20GLP-1%20users%20want,and%20sandwich%2Fdelis%20down%204.5%25) [255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [75](https://www.circana.com/post/chain-reactions-the-impact-of-glp-1-usage-on-non-food-categories#:~:text=Taking%20it%20personally%3A%20Circana%E2%80%99s,usage%20compared%20to%20non-users)

### 10.1. Summary of evidence across categories

Across the report, the strongest cross-category pattern is selective demand compression rather than generalized food collapse. Household-level evidence shows that GLP-1 adoption reduces grocery spending by 5.3% within six months, with larger reductions of about 8.2% among higher-income households, while category-level declines are concentrated in chips and savory snacks, sweet bakery, cookies, and other calorie-dense processed foods.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Using%20a%20combination%20of,8.2%25%20among%20higher-income%20households) [158](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/suppl/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=We%20begin%20with%20an,grocery%20spending%20following%20adoption)

- Demand losers: The most exposed categories are those dependent on appetite frequency, impulse consumption, and attachment purchases. Public 2026 market summaries show QSR spending down 8.7% per GLP-1 household after 6 to 8 months, with especially sharp declines in pizza, chicken, coffee and bakery, and burgers.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=What%20GLP-1%20users%20want,and%20sandwich%2Fdelis%20down%204.5%25)
- Demand winners: The most resilient and advantaged categories are those that deliver protein, satiety, digestive tolerance, hydration, and nutrient density in smaller formats. NIQ reported that GLP-1 users, despite representing 4.5% of households in one panel cut, drove 43% of sales growth for products with muscle health claims across consecutive 52-week periods in 2024 and 2025.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value)
- Basket redesign: Circana and NIQ both frame the effect as evolving baskets rather than simple appetite disappearance, meaning spend is being reallocated toward protein snacks, yogurt, fresh produce, supplements, and health-management products rather than removed one-for-one from the consumer wallet.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased) [125](https://www.circana.com/insights-hub/glp-1s-consumer-behavior#:~:text=The%206%20Most%20Surprising,Circana%E2%80%99s%20Latest%20GLP-1%20Research) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value)
- Clinical to commercial bridge: Nutrition guidance from four major professional organizations emphasizes adequate protein, micronutrient sufficiency, management of gastrointestinal side effects, and minimally processed nutrient-dense diets, which aligns closely with the categories showing relative strength in retail data.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [271](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12264624/#:~:text=15%3A100181.%20doi%3A%2010.1016%2Fj.obpill.2025.100181%23%20Nutritional,and%20the%20Obesity%20Society)

Taken together, the evidence indicates that GLP-1 drugs are reshaping food demand through both lower total intake and higher selectivity per eating occasion. For industry professionals and investors, the key takeaway is that category exposure now depends less on broad food inflation or consumer confidence alone and more on whether a product earns its place in a smaller, more intentional basket.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Using%20a%20combination%20of,8.2%25%20among%20higher-income%20households) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased)

### 10.2. What the data suggests about the permanence of these shifts

The available data supports a view of GLP-1-driven demand change as structurally important but not mechanically permanent in every component. The strongest evidence for durability comes from rising household penetration, sustained category-level divergence in scanner data, and forward projections that GLP-1 households could represent 35% of U.S. food and beverage sales or units by 2030, depending on the Circana framing cited, which implies that this consumer segment will remain too large to ignore even if adherence is imperfect.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased) [75](https://www.circana.com/post/chain-reactions-the-impact-of-glp-1-usage-on-non-food-categories#:~:text=Taking%20it%20personally%3A%20Circana%E2%80%99s,usage%20compared%20to%20non-users)

- Evidence for persistence: PwC reported that as of May 2026, 21% of U.S. households included a current GLP-1 user, up from 9% in January 2025, indicating that the installed base has already reached a scale where household routines, pantry norms, and restaurant choices can change beyond the treated individual.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=What%20GLP-1%20users%20want,and%20sandwich%2Fdelis%20down%204.5%25)
- Evidence against full permanence: The household spending literature indicates that the demand effect is strongest during active use and can moderate over time, while the underlying mechanism of appetite suppression is medication-dependent. This means some portion of reduced calorie demand is reversible if treatment stops or access weakens.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Using%20a%20combination%20of,8.2%25%20among%20higher-income%20households) [158](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/suppl/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=We%20begin%20with%20an,grocery%20spending%20following%20adoption)
- Why the shift still looks structural: Circana explicitly argues that market impact can remain sustained as consumers move in and out of active medication use but retain some health-conscious behaviors adopted during treatment. That interpretation is reinforced by the industry’s own response, including reformulation, GLP-1-oriented merchandising, and menu redesign, which are typically signs of expected persistence rather than a short-lived fad.[28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased) [75](https://www.circana.com/post/chain-reactions-the-impact-of-glp-1-usage-on-non-food-categories#:~:text=Taking%20it%20personally%3A%20Circana%E2%80%99s,usage%20compared%20to%20non-users)
- Most defensible conclusion: The permanent element is likely to be the direction of demand reallocation, not the exact magnitude at every point in time. In practical terms, fewer indulgent eating occasions and greater preference for protein, hydration, and nutrient density appear durable, while the speed and depth of category disruption will remain sensitive to reimbursement, affordability, discontinuation, and future label expansions.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value) [255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=What%20GLP-1%20users%20want,and%20sandwich%2Fdelis%20down%204.5%25)

For strategic planning, the correct framing is therefore structural trend with variable amplitude. Companies that wait for perfect permanence proof risk underreacting to a demand modifier that is already visible in household spending, category mix, and innovation pipelines as of August 25, 2026.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Using%20a%20combination%20of,8.2%25%20among%20higher-income%20households) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value)

### 10.3. Recommendations for industry adaptation

Industry adaptation should focus on redesigning relevance for a consumer who is eating less, choosing more deliberately, and expecting more nutritional utility from each purchase. The strategic objective is not simply to defend volume, but to reposition portfolios, assortments, and menu economics around satiety, protein adequacy, digestive comfort, hydration, and portion appropriateness.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased)

- Manufacturers: Re-rank innovation pipelines toward protein-forward, fiber-aware, micronutrient-supportive, and portion-controlled products. Claims should be tied to credible nutritional architecture rather than generic wellness language, especially because professional guidance now emphasizes protein adequacy, minimally processed diets, and side-effect-aware eating patterns.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [271](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12264624/#:~:text=15%3A100181.%20doi%3A%2010.1016%2Fj.obpill.2025.100181%23%20Nutritional,and%20the%20Obesity%20Society)
- Retailers: Rebuild assortment and adjacency around mission-based baskets, such as protein snack plus hydration, yogurt plus digestive health, or fresh convenience plus supplements. Scanner evidence suggests that GLP-1 influence is strongest where consumers are making fewer but more intentional choices, so shelf strategy should reduce search friction for these missions.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value) [125](https://www.circana.com/insights-hub/glp-1s-consumer-behavior#:~:text=The%206%20Most%20Surprising,Circana%E2%80%99s%20Latest%20GLP-1%20Research)
- Restaurants and QSR: Assume mix pressure before traffic pressure. Menu engineering should prioritize smaller portions, modular protein add-ons, lower-waste formats, and beverage and side strategies that fit reduced appetite rather than relying on legacy combo economics.[139](https://www.pwc.com/us/en/industries/consumer-markets/library/glp-1-consumer-trends.html#:~:text=What%20GLP-1%20users%20want,and%20sandwich%2Fdelis%20down%204.5%25)
- Investors: Treat GLP-1 exposure as a recurring portfolio screen. Assets concentrated in indulgent snacks, sugary beverages, and attachment-heavy foodservice deserve higher scrutiny, while assets with credible exposure to protein, functional hydration, yogurt, produce, and supplement-adjacent categories warrant strategic premium consideration.[189](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/00222437251412834#:~:text=Using%20a%20combination%20of,8.2%25%20among%20higher-income%20households) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased) [30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value)
- Governance and labeling: Build internal standards for any GLP-1-related positioning, including minimum protein thresholds, transparent macro disclosure, and dietitian or clinical review. The market opportunity is real, but so is the credibility risk if products are marketed as GLP-1-friendly without clear nutritional substantiation.[255](https://www.obesity.org/nutritional-priorities-to-support-glp-1-therapy-for-obesity/#:~:text=The%20advisory%20outlines%20eight,practical%2C%20evidence-informed%20nutrition%20strategies.%E2%80%9D) [271](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12264624/#:~:text=15%3A100181.%20doi%3A%2010.1016%2Fj.obpill.2025.100181%23%20Nutritional,and%20the%20Obesity%20Society)

The central recommendation is to adapt now, but adapt with precision. The winners in a GLP-1-influenced market are unlikely to be the companies that merely cut calories or add a label, but those that redesign products, pricing, merchandising, and menu architecture around the economics of intentional appetite.[30](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/insights/analysis/2026/health-and-wellness-consumer-trends-the-rise-of-the-self-directed-health-consumer/#:~:text=Early%20retail%20concerns%20about,size%20and%20trip%20value) [28](https://www.circana.com/post/glp-1-medication-users-to-represent-35-percent-of-u-s-food-and-beverage-sales-by-2030#:~:text=2025%20%E2%80%93%20The%20rapid,at%20restaurants%20has%20increased)
