## Standing rules

**Never publish these** — in any asset, caption, subtitle, file name or ad:

- **"LAM" or "Large Analysis Model"**
- Any source count other than **25M+**
- **"SOC 2 certified"** — the only permitted wording is *"SOC 2 Type I audit in progress"*
- Exclamation points
- *platform · leverages · algorithms · workflows · powerful AI · revolutionary · game-changing · chatbot · web scraping · hallucinate · "excited to announce"*

**Two rules that come with the positioning:**

**Never name a competitor or AI product in lead copy.** Naming them puts Caspr in their category, which is the one thing the positioning exists to avoid. The contrast belongs in comparison pages and social, never in a headline, hero or ad.

**Never ask the reader to do work.** "Verify", "check the sources", "see for yourself" all sell more labour and contradict the promise. The evidence is shown, never assigned.

**Voice** is the Trusted Senior Analyst: precise, authoritative, calm. Conclusions rather than descriptions. Numbers rather than adjectives — *"15 minutes"* not *"fast"*.
