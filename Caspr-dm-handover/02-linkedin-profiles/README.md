# LinkedIn — the seven individual profiles

**Owner:** each person, coordinated by the Social Specialist
**Time:** 5 minutes each, plus ~20 minutes a week ongoing
**Detail, rewrites and reasoning:** [`audit.md`](audit.md)

---

## Why this matters more than it looks

Combined, the seven profiles reach **31,483 followers** — over a hundred times the Company Page. They are the largest owned audience Caspr has, and most of the reach the company gets will come through them rather than the Page.

---

## What to do

### One-off, this week

1. **Everyone selects the same Caspr Company Page** from the dropdown, so it links rather than free-texts. Right now the company is written three different ways across five profiles, which splits the follower graph and means no page accumulates authority.
2. **Joy adds Caspr as a concurrent current position and sets it to display.** Joy carries both brands by decision — see `audit.md` §2.2 for how LinkedIn handles that, because it does it badly by default.
3. **Everyone applies their new headline** from `audit.md` §4.
4. **Founders connect with Kartikey** — he currently shows as a 2nd-degree connection.

### Ongoing, weekly

Each person publishes in their own lane and leaves **two substantive comments** — one on a team post, one external. Lanes and cadence are in `audit.md` §5.

> **No coordinated applause.** Every comment adds a fact, a number, a counter-example or a real question from that person's own domain. If a comment could have been written by someone who had not read the post, it does not go out. Five colleagues saying "Great post" is visible astroturf, and it costs more than the reach is worth.

---

## Before anything is posted

The Company Page still carries live posts using **"Large Analysis Model (LAM)"** and **"1 million curated sources"**. Both are prohibited. Task **01** removes them — do that first, so nothing new drives traffic to them.
