## Why this is the highest-value thing on the list

The single biggest barrier to conversion is whether a buyer believes AI-produced research is credible enough to put in front of a board. No copy answers that. A practitioner with twenty years at Kantar, Nielsen, GfK or Euromonitor saying it on camera does.

Sixteen such people are reachable through the SPOC. The marginal cost is a video call and an editor's afternoon.

---

## Two things to hold onto

**Quality is the point.** A grainy webcam clip undermines the exact claim it is making. §6 of the brief specifies the recording setup end to end — follow it, and run the technical check call, which is what separates ten usable recordings from six.

**The by-products matter more than the videos.** Ten interviews yield 80+ cleared written quotes, 50+ stills, and ten long-form transcripts. That library feeds the website, ads, decks and email for two years. The video is the visible output; the text is the one that gets used most.
