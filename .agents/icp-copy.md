### Rules

1. **Never name a competitor or the LLM category in lead copy.** The contrast belongs on `/vs/*` and in social, never in a hero, headline or ad.
2. **Never frame proof as work for the reader.** No "verify", "check", "traceable" as an instruction. Describe the artefact; never assign the labour.
3. **Cost is the third act.** Proof lines and ad creative only.
4. **No tier names without explanation.** *Brief*, *Study* and *Intelligence* are Caspr vocabulary. A first-time reader has never seen them — say what they get, not what it's called.

### How these were written

That is the method: eight people with eight different wounds, in their own words.
