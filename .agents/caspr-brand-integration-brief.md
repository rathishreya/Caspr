# Caspr / Ghost Research — Brand Integration Guidelines

*Prepared: May 2026*
*For: Caspr Branding Team*

---

## Context

Caspr and Ghost Research (GR) share the same underlying technology but serve different buyers under different value propositions. GR positions as expert-validated market research — the AI engine is deliberately in the background. Caspr is the AI engine itself, sold as a standalone product to a different audience. The two brands are complementary but must remain visually and verbally separate.

---

## The Governing Rule: One-Directional

GR never references Caspr in any buyer-facing material. Caspr may reference GR's production outcomes — but without naming GR.

**Why this matters:** If GR buyers can trace their reports back to a standalone AI product, they bypass the expert-validation layer and GR's pricing rationale collapses. The connection should be inferable to a sophisticated observer but is never advertised.

---

## What Caspr Marketing Can Say

Use GR's production metrics as proof of Caspr's real-world scale:

> *"Caspr is already deployed in production. One research publisher using our engine has generated 500+ expert-validated sector reports across 12 industries and 6 geographies, validated by 1,000+ domain practitioners. That's the baseline the Caspr API is built from."*

This positions Caspr as proven at scale without exposing the brand connection.

---

## What to Avoid

- Do not name Ghost Research in any Caspr marketing material
- Do not describe the relationship as "powered by," "sister brand," or "parent company"
- Do not link caspr.ai → ghostresearch.com or vice versa
- Do not use GR report screenshots or product UI in Caspr marketing assets

---

## Long-Term Evolution

Once Caspr has additional customers, GR naturally becomes "one of several production deployments" — anonymised by volume. Until then, use the aggregated metric framing above. At that point, the founder LinkedIn can reference GR more openly as a proof case without the connection being uniquely traceable.
