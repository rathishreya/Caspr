UK Logistics Technology Market
01 Three players control 58% of last-mile routing software — the market is more concentrated than it appears

The UK logistics-tech market generated £4.2 billion in 2025, but headline market size obscures a structural reality: routing and dispatch software — the most commercially critical segment — is controlled by three vendors. Samsara, Trimble, and RouteMaster collectively hold 58% of UK active deployments across fleet sizes above 50 vehicles. The long tail of 200+ smaller vendors competes for the remaining 42%, primarily in niche verticals or geographies where the enterprise vendors have not yet moved aggressively.

02 Warehouse automation is driven by labour cost pressure, not capability — and this changes the buyer conversation

UK warehouse automation spend increased 34% year-on-year in 2025, reaching £1.1 billion. The primary driver is not technological maturity — automation capability in conveyance, picking, and sortation has existed for over a decade. The decisive change is a 22% increase in minimum wage costs since 2022, which has shifted automated picking from a 7.1-year payback to a 3.8-year payback for operations above 50,000 sq ft. Below that threshold, the economics remain marginal. Vendors that lead with capability messaging are solving the wrong problem — buyers in 2026 are purchasing a labour cost hedge, not a technology upgrade.

UK Logistics Technology Market
03 PE-backed consolidation has accelerated 40% in 12 months — the window for independent positioning is closing

Fourteen UK logistics-tech acquisitions were completed in the 12 months to June 2026, up from ten in the prior period. Eight of the fourteen were PE-backed roll-up transactions, clustering in two segments: last-mile routing (five deals) and warehouse management systems (four deals). Average EV/Revenue multiples compressed from 6.2× to 4.8× as deal volume increased — a growing seller pool giving acquirers pricing power. Two of the eight PE acquirers have made public statements of intent for further consolidation within 18 months. Independent vendors with revenue below £5 million are the primary acquisition targets; those with proprietary data assets command a 30–40% premium to the prevailing multiple.

KEY TAKEAWAYS
Three vendors control last-mile routing — any market entry or partnership strategy must account for a structurally concentrated buying environment where the top three set category expectations
Warehouse automation economics crossed the viability threshold in 2024; the growth window is now, not emerging — vendors entering in 2025–26 are entering at peak demand, not early
PE consolidation is active and accelerating — independent vendors face approximately a two-year window before market structure changes materially and competitive positioning resets
