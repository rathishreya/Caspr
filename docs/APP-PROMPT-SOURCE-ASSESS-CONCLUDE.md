## 1 · ⛔ The Thinking Brain and the Learning Brain are retired, together

**Source:** [`source-assess-conclude.md`](source-assess-conclude.md)

> ### Source. — every source, credible.
> ### Assess. — every claim, triangulated.
> ### Conclude. — every report, defensible.

**Both brains go. The anatomical frame was the problem, not the adjectives** — *thinking* is now a loading
state on every frontier model, and *learning* was never accurate.

## 4 · The report cover — coordinate, do not act alone

**The provenance mark on every generated PDF currently reads:**

> *"Analysis generated by Caspr's Thinking Brain; every claim cited to source."*

**It becomes:**

> *"Sourced, assessed and concluded by Caspr; every claim cited to source."*

**Better regardless** — it says what was done rather than which organ did it.

**⚠ Two reasons this is not purely a design change.** It is on **all 23 delivered reports**, and it is half of
a legal requirement — **EU AI Act Article 50(2)** demands a visible mark *and* a machine-readable one, and
the **XMP half is currently absent entirely** (`report-evaluation-2026-08.md` §5.1). **Change the visible
string with Jayant, in the same pass that adds the XMP fields.** Changing one without the other leaves the
compliance gap open and makes it look addressed.

## 6 · How to work

**Load the `figma-use` skill before every `use_figma` call**, work in small atomic steps, and **return the node
IDs of everything you touch.**

**Do not rebuild what exists.** Clone and edit — the standing rule in this project is that hand-rebuilding a
component that already exists is how variants multiply. The header sweep found **seven button variants and
`Log in` in three different colours** across 25 frames; that is what rebuilding produces.
