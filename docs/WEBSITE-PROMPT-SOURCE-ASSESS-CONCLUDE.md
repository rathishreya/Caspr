# Website prompt — the architecture rename and what moved with it

*Paste into the website session. **This is a delta, not a re-brief*** — `website-session-prompt.md` still
governs everything it covers. **Four decisions landed after it was written, and one of them changes the proof
line you have been building against.**

---

## 1 · ⛔ The Thinking Brain and the Learning Brain are retired, together

**Source:** [`source-assess-conclude.md`](source-assess-conclude.md) — read it before touching copy.

> ### Source. — every source, credible.
> ### Assess. — every claim, triangulated.
> ### Conclude. — every report, defensible.

**Three stages, and they *are* the proof line** — the promise and the mechanism are one asset, not two things
a reader has to remember.

**⚠ The proof line itself changed.** Two of its three clauses moved:

| Was | Now |
|---|---|
| Every source, credible | **unchanged** |
| ~~Every alternative, weighed~~ | **Every claim, triangulated** |
| ~~Every conclusion, defensible~~ | **Every report, defensible** |

**Why, so you do not re-litigate it:** the clauses were not parallel — the stage word was the noun in two and
an adjective in the third. **The units now nest: source → claim → report.** And *claim* is the unit we
actually measure: **355–678 cited claims per report**, so the line is countable rather than rhetorical.
Full reasoning and every rejected alternative: §2A of the source doc.

### ⛔ Never write

**Thinking Brain · Learning Brain · brain · thinking · reasoning · cognitive** — anywhere near the
architecture. *Thinking* is now a loading state on every frontier model; using it says *we do what every
chatbot does.*

## 4 · The access model — plan names and two framing rules

**Source:** [`product/access-model.md`](product/access-model.md)

**`Try · Solo · Team · Org` replace Professional / Business / Enterprise everywhere.**

| ⛔ Never | ✅ Always |
|---|---|
| *from $200/mo* · *$200 per month* · *the $200 plan* | **$200 a month of research** — `COPY-11a` |
| ***"only what you run is recharged"*** · *"only pay for what you use"* · *"never charged for value you did not receive"* | **unused balance carries forward** · **each month restores only what you consumed** — `COPY-11b` |

**⚠ The second row is a factual correction, not a tonal one.** `R = F + top_up`, and **the platform fee is
charged every month regardless of use** — $14 on a $200 budget even at zero consumption. **The old phrasing
was false and has been swept from the deck.**
