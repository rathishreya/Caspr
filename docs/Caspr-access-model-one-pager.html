Access model · Internal

What it costs, and whose budget pays.

Analyses are $15, $80 or $300 by depth — the same at every level. What commitment changes is what comes with them. Then one rule decides every debit in the product.

The four modes

Named for how you work, not for what you spend.

  • TryNo commitment$100 to start, no card. Then top up when you need to. Every depth, every output on that depth's menu, Ask Caspr and every citation.
  • Solo$200 / researchOne professional working alone. Adds editing, Updates, and sharing an analysis for others to read.
  • Team$600 / researchRecommended. Adds your own Data Room, and colleagues who can edit alongside you.
  • Org$1,800 committedThree seats minimum. One pooled budget instead of three that strand. Org-wide Data Room, SSO, API, Projects. Residency and deployment are quoted.

Two gates only — editing at Solo, Data Room and collaborative editing at Team. Nothing is gated that costs nothing to serve.

Generation against an analysis debits whoever owns that analysis.

Starting an analysis debits you — held at the Gate, settled on delivery, released if it fails. Editing, follow-ups, extra formats and extra languages on an analysis shared with you debit the owner, never you. Reading a shared analysis and every citation in it debit nobody: Ask Caspr is never metered, at any level, including an expired trial.

Two pots, no bleed. Editing an owner's analysis always draws the owner's wallet — and a new collaborator's own $100 stays reserved for their first question, not spent on somebody else's document. An editor cannot start an analysis on another account.

Files do not travel unless you send them.

A shared analysis carries every citation to a public source. Files from your Data Room travel only on an explicit, per-share grant that defaults to no. A claim resting on an ungranted file renders as the claim, the source named as private, and no content — sourced without being exposed.

The monthly charge is the fee plus what you spent.

On a $200 budget the cap is $186 and the fee is $14. Run nothing and next month is $14. Run one $80 analysis and it is $94 — eighty to restore the balance, plus the fee. The fee is charged every month regardless of use. Org is the exception: committed spend, charged in full, used or lost.

How to say it.

Never "$200 a month" as though it were a fee — it is money set aside to spend on your own research. Say "$200 a month of research", and pair it with "unused balance carries forward".

Never "you're only charged for what you run" — the fee is charged whether you run anything or not, so that one is simply false. And never name a depth without its type: Brief / Study / Intelligence is the Market Research ladder; Investment & Deal runs Screen / Thesis / Diligence. For the rung across all types, say "the $300 depth".
