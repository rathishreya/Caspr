## 1. Concept

The metaphor is "The pitch document that wins the room." An agency strategist recognises this immediately: it is the category brief that walks into a credentials meeting fully loaded — market numbers, consumer insight, competitive positioning, and a clear strategic frame. No waiting for the research team. No junior analyst with a half-built deck. The camera moves the way a creative director reads a brief: quickly enough to assess, slowly enough to absorb. Dark background, near-silence, no narration. Aluminum containers, bamboo, natural soap — the cover signals that this is real category territory, not a generic business report. The emotional register is not excitement. It is the quiet satisfaction of arriving at a pitch already armed.

## 2. Format Specs

| Parameter | Spec |
|---|---|
| Duration | 25–28 seconds (do not cut shorter — the pacing is deliberate) |
| Primary aspect ratio | 16:9 — 1920×1080 |
| Vertical cut | 9:16 — 1080×1920 (mobile / social) |
| Frame rate | 30fps |
| Primary format | MP4, H.264 |
| Web format | WebM (for autoplay performance on Chrome/Firefox) |
| Audio — Version A | Silent (web autoplay version — primary delivery) |

## 4. Style Guide

### Background
`#0B0B09` — near-black with warm undertone. Consistent across all shots.

### Document Presentation
- Pages at ~75% canvas width, centred
- Drop shadow: `0px 8px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- No device mockup, no browser chrome

### Typography (overlays only)
- Typeface: Inter
- Sizes: 11–14pt
- Colour: #F5F2EF at 60–80% opacity
- Weight: Regular for metadata; Semi Bold for end card
- No drop shadow on text

### Red Accent Usage
- Colour: #E8453C
- Uses: TOC rule, segment highlight (8%), brand table bars (50%), wordmark pulse
- Never a bold graphic

### Transitions
- Cross-dissolve only, 0.3–0.5s (except the Shot 2→3 cut)
- No wipes, slides, or 3D effects

### Pacing
- Shots 1–5: slow, deliberate
- Shot 6: fast
- Total: 26–28 seconds

### What to Avoid
- Cursor, browser chrome, loading indicators
- Upbeat or motivational music
- Tutorial framing
- Explanatory captions
