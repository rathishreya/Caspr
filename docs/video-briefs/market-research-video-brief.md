## 1. Concept

The metaphor is "The primary research brief that arrives without the fieldwork." A senior market researcher, insights director, or consumer intelligence lead recognises this immediately: it is the structured consumer brief that normally takes six weeks, a panel, a debrief, and a 90-slide deck — delivered in 15 minutes with every claim cited. UK consumer segmentation. BNPL adoption curves. Embedded finance awareness and intent data broken down by demographic. The kind of output that justifies a retainer. The camera moves the way a research director reads a brief: structure first, methodology second, data third. Dark background, near-silence, no narration. A tablet displaying an embedded finance or BNPL interface in a retail context, with a UK flag credit card or contactless symbol in the scene, grounds the viewer in the research category immediately. The emotional register is not excitement. It is the professional satisfaction of presenting findings already verified, structured, and ready to cite.

## 4. Style Guide

### Background
`#0B0B09` — near-black with warm undertone. Consistent across all shots.

### Document Presentation
- Pages at ~75% canvas width, centred
- Drop shadow: `0px 8px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- No device mockup, no browser chrome

### Typography (overlays only)
- Typeface: Inter
- Sizes: 11–14pt
- Colour: #F5F2EF at 60–80% opacity
- Weight: Regular for metadata; Semi Bold for end card
- No drop shadow on text

### Red Accent Usage
- Colour: #E8453C
- Uses: TOC rule, demographic highlight (8%), provider table bars (50%), wordmark pulse
- Never a bold graphic

### Transitions
- Cross-dissolve only, 0.3–0.5s (except the Shot 2→3 cut)
- No wipes, slides, or 3D effects

### Pacing
- Shots 1–5: slow, deliberate
- Shot 6: fast
- Total: 26–28 seconds

### What to Avoid
- Cursor, browser chrome, loading indicators
- Upbeat or motivational music
- Tutorial framing
- Explanatory captions
