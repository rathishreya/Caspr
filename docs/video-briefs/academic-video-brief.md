## 1. Concept

The metaphor is "The literature review that was done before you arrived." An MBA student, MSc researcher, or doctoral candidate recognises this immediately: it is the market context chapter that takes three weeks to write — investment flow data by geography, technology subsector breakdown, policy and regulatory landscape, capital deployment trends, and a structured thesis on where the opportunity is. The camera moves the way a serious researcher reads: methodically, without sentiment, with the deliberate pace of someone building an argument rather than browsing a news feed. Dark background, near-silence, no narration. Renewable energy infrastructure in an emerging market geography — solar arrays, wind installations, construction at scale — grounds the cover in the physical reality of capital deployment. This is not an advocacy image. It is the operational evidence behind an investment data thesis. The emotional register is not inspiration. It is the particular clarity of having the research done before the first seminar.

## 4. Style Guide

### Background
`#0B0B09` — near-black with warm undertone. Consistent across all shots.

### Document Presentation
- Pages at ~75% canvas width, centred
- Drop shadow: `0px 8px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- No device mockup, no browser chrome

### Typography (overlays only)
- Typeface: Inter
- Sizes: 11–14pt
- Colour: #F5F2EF at 60–80% opacity
- Weight: Regular for metadata; Semi Bold for end card
- No drop shadow on text

### Red Accent Usage
- Colour: #E8453C
- Uses: TOC rule, region/subsector highlight (8%), investor table bars (50%), wordmark pulse
- Never a bold graphic

### Transitions
- Cross-dissolve only, 0.3–0.5s (except the Shot 2→3 cut)
- No wipes, slides, or 3D effects

### Pacing
- Shots 1–5: slow, deliberate
- Shot 6: fast
- Total: 26–28 seconds

### What to Avoid
- Cursor, browser chrome, loading indicators
- Upbeat or motivational music
- Tutorial framing
- Explanatory captions
