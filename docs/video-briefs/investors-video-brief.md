## 1. Concept

The metaphor is "The due diligence package that precedes a decision." A PE principal or VC partner recognises this document as the research that lives in the deal folder — market sizing, competitor mapping, valuation comps, investment thesis validation. The camera moves with the unhurried confidence of a fund that has already screened the sector. Dark background, near-silence, no narration. A European corporate environment — the exterior of a financial district office, a formal boardroom, or professionals at work — grounds the cover in institutional seriousness. The emotional register is not excitement. It is the deliberate calm of someone who does this before writing a cheque.

## 4. Style Guide

### Background
`#0B0B09` — near-black with a warm undertone. Not pure black (#000000). Consistent across all shots.

### Document Presentation
- Pages at approximately 75% canvas width, centred
- Drop shadow: `0px 8px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- No border, frame, or device mockup
- Pages read as documents, not software screenshots

### Typography (overlays only)
- Typeface: Inter
- Sizes: 11–14pt
- Colour: #F5F2EF at 60–80% opacity
- Weight: Regular for metadata; Semi Bold for end card only
- No drop shadow on text overlays

### Red Accent Usage
- Colour: #E8453C
- Uses: TOC rule (Shot 2), peak year row highlight (Shot 3, 8% opacity), landscape accent bars (Shot 5, 50% opacity), wordmark dot pulse (End Card)
- Never a bold graphic element

### Transitions
- Cross-dissolve only, 0.3–0.5s
- Deliberate cut between Shot 2 and Shot 3 is the single exception
- No wipes, slides, 3D turns, or motion blur

### Pacing
- Shots 1–5: slow and deliberate
- Shot 6: fast — contrast and volume
- End card: readable pace
- Total: 26–28 seconds

### What to Avoid
- Cursor movements, browser chrome, typing animations, loading bars
- Upbeat music or motivational tone
- Product tutorial framing
- Explanatory captions on data tables
