# Caspr — Video Production Brief: Category Managers

**Date:** 16 May 2026
**Report subject:** European Plant-Based Food Category Report 2024 (Study-level output, 72 pages)
**Placement:** ICP landing page — category managers / retail & eCommerce — output showcase
**Status:** Ready for production

---

## 1. Concept

The metaphor is "The range review that already has the answers." A category manager or buyer at a retail group or eCommerce platform recognises this immediately: it is the market intelligence that should have been on the desk before the last ranging decision — category size by country, consumer segmentation by purchase driver, competitive shelf map, private label opportunity. The camera moves the way a head of buying reads a category brief: quickly scanning for the number, slowing to absorb the insight. Dark background, near-silence, no narration. Vivid plant-based food photography — oat milk, meat alternatives, fresh vegetables, minimal packaging — fills the cover. Not a health-and-wellness mood board. A data-anchored commercial brief about a category growing faster than almost anything else on the planogram. The emotional register is not excitement. It is the quiet authority of someone who walked into the range review already knowing which SKUs to cut.

## 4. Style Guide

### Background
`#0B0B09` — near-black with warm undertone. Consistent across all shots.

### Document Presentation
- Pages at ~75% canvas width, centred
- Drop shadow: `0px 8px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- No device mockup, no browser chrome

### Typography (overlays only)
- Typeface: Inter
- Sizes: 11–14pt
- Colour: #F5F2EF at 60–80% opacity
- Weight: Regular for metadata; Semi Bold for end card
- No drop shadow on text

### Red Accent Usage
- Colour: #E8453C
- Uses: TOC rule, subcategory highlight (8%), brand table bars (50%), wordmark pulse
- Never a bold graphic

### Transitions
- Cross-dissolve only, 0.3–0.5s (except the Shot 2→3 cut)
- No wipes, slides, or 3D effects

### Pacing
- Shots 1–5: slow, deliberate
- Shot 6: fast
- Total: 26–28 seconds

### What to Avoid
- Cursor, browser chrome, loading indicators
- Upbeat or motivational music
- Tutorial framing
- Explanatory captions
