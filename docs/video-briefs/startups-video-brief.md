## 1. Concept

The metaphor is "Investor-readiness arriving — the market sizing that answers the VC question." A startup founder at Series A or Series B recognises this immediately: it is the market research that lives in the data room and gets cited in the pitch deck. TAM, SAM, SOM. Competitive landscape. Growth projections. The questions every VC asks and every founder scrambles to answer convincingly. The camera moves with the unhurried precision of a founder who already has the numbers. Dark background, near-silence, no narration. Commercial logistics infrastructure — trucks at a distribution depot, a fleet operations dispatch centre, or the physical scale of the industry this software manages — grounds the cover in operational reality. The emotional register is not excitement. It is the particular confidence of walking into a partner meeting with the market sized, the competitors mapped, and the thesis already written.

## 4. Style Guide

### Background
`#0B0B09` — near-black with warm undertone. Consistent across all shots.

### Document Presentation
- Pages at ~75% canvas width, centred
- Drop shadow: `0px 8px 40px rgba(0,0,0,0.6)`
- No device mockup, no browser chrome

### Typography (overlays only)
- Typeface: Inter
- Sizes: 11–14pt
- Colour: #F5F2EF at 60–80% opacity
- Weight: Regular for metadata; Semi Bold for end card
- No drop shadow on text

### Red Accent Usage
- Colour: #E8453C
- Uses: TOC rule, segment highlight (8%), vendor table bars (50%), wordmark pulse
- Never a bold graphic

### Transitions
- Cross-dissolve only, 0.3–0.5s (except the Shot 2→3 cut)
- No wipes, slides, or 3D effects

### Pacing
- Shots 1–5: slow, deliberate
- Shot 6: fast
- Total: 26–28 seconds

### What to Avoid
- Cursor, browser chrome, loading indicators
- Upbeat or motivational music
- Tutorial framing
- Explanatory captions
