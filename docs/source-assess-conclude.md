# Source. Assess. Conclude.

**Locked 2026-08-27 (Joy). Replaces the Thinking Brain and the Learning Brain, together.**
**This file is the source. Everything else points here.**

---

## 1 · The decision

> ### Source. — every source, credible.
> ### Assess. — every claim, triangulated.
> ### Conclude. — every report, defensible.

**Three stages. They are the proof line, restated as an architecture** — so the promise and the mechanism are
one asset rather than two things to remember.

| | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Source** | What comes in. The **25M+ curated, credible corpus**, the **live feeds** that keep it current, and **your own material** — uploads, Data Room, prior analyses |
| **Assess** | Establishing each claim against the sources. **Triangulating across independent sources, separating a definitional difference from a genuine one, deciding which figure answers the question asked** |
| **Conclude** | Structuring a report that survives the room. **A position, not a summary** |

## 2 · ⛔ Retired: the Thinking Brain and the Learning Brain

**Both. Together. The anatomical frame was the problem, not the adjectives.**

### Why *Thinking* had to go

**"Thinking" is no longer a capability. It is a loading state.** Every frontier model renders *Thinking…* above
a spinner. **So "the Thinking Brain" now says *we do what every chatbot does*** — the precise opposite of
*Not an assistant. An analyst.*

**Our differentiating architecture name had been colonised by the category we decline to be in.**

### Why *Learning* went with it

**It was never accurate.** Nothing learns — the system ingests live data and retains context about your work.
*Learning* is machine-learning vocabulary describing something that is not happening.

**And it was doing two jobs at once** — the world's currency *and* knowledge of the customer. **Both now live
inside Source**, where they belong: they are things that come in, not things that are done.

## 2A · Why the middle word is *Assess* and not *Weigh* — settled 2026-08-27

**An earlier version of this decision used *Weigh*, and the proof line read *"every alternative, weighed."*
Two faults, both raised by Joy:**

| | |
|---|---|
| **1 · The clauses were not parallel** | In *every **source**, credible* and *every **conclusion**, defensible* the stage word is the **noun**. In *every alternative, **weighed*** it was the **adjective**, and the noun was a stranger. **Set in type three times, the asymmetry shows** |
| **2 · *Weigh* leans on *weights*** | Mild, but real — *weights* is core ML vocabulary. **Having just retired *thinking* for being colonised, adopting a second contested word would be the same mistake twice** |

### And the units now nest

> **source → claim → report**

**Sources support claims; claims compose the report.** Each unit is larger than the last and the guarantee
escalates with it. **That is a better organising logic than making each stage word appear twice.**

**Two further decisions inside that:**

- **The unit is the *claim*, because the claim is what we measured.** 355–678 cited claims per report — **the
  proof line's unit and the evidence's unit are the same thing**, so it is countable rather than rhetorical
- **Defensibility broadened from the conclusion to the report.** What a buyer carries into a room is the whole
  document, not its final line

### ⛔ Words considered and rejected

| | Why not |
|---|---|
| **Judge** | *"Too judgemental"* — an analyst assesses evidence; you judge people |
| **Analyse** | **It is the whole, not a part.** Caspr *is* Analytical AI — making it one third asks what the other two are. Also 1-3-2 on the beat |
| **Prove** | **Contradicts our own doctrine.** We surface disagreement rather than settling it — *"a reconciled framework should not force one 'correct' number."* Also advertising-flagged, and it breaks the escalation: *proven* overshoots *defensible* |
| **Scientific** *(as the quality)* | Claims a standard we do not meet — hypothesis, falsifiability, reproducibility. **Our own corpus brief concedes runs vary.** Checkable in ninety seconds, and it fails the check |
| **Test · Check** | We do not examine pre-existing claims. **We make them** |
| **Triangulate** *(as the stage)* | Excellent meaning, unusable form — 1-4-2, and *"every triangulation"* is not idiomatic. **Kept as the quality word instead**, where it is |
| **Establish** | The most precise verb available, and three syllables. **The better word and the worse line** |

## 3 · The mapping — because it is not one-to-one

**⚠ Never find-and-replace this. The two brains do not map onto the three stages cleanly.**

| Old | New |
|---|---|
| **The Learning Brain** — live data, curated corpus, customer context | → **Source** |
| **The Thinking Brain** — *"reasons through data, structures conclusions"* | → **splits.** Reasoning is **Assess**; structuring the report is **Conclude** |

**The most common sentence in the old copy** — *"the Thinking Brain reasons through data and structures
conclusions"* — **is two stages, not one.** Rewrite it as two.

## 4 · How to write it

| Context | Use |
|---|---|
| **Full lockup** — deck, homepage, how-it-works | The three lines in §1, verbs paired with their proof-line clause |
| **Short form** — anywhere without room | **Source. Assess. Conclude.** Three words, three full stops |
| **In a sentence** | *"Caspr sources, assesses and concludes."* Removes any doubt about who is doing the work |
| **Report provenance line** | *"Sourced, assessed and concluded by Caspr."* **Better than the old line** — it says what was done, not which organ did it |

### ⛔ Do not

| | |
|---|---|
| **Do not name the whole thing** | No *Caspr Method*, no *The Three*, no coined collective noun. **Every capitalised term is credibility you have to fund**, and three verbs that stand on their own do not need a fourth word above them. *"How Caspr works"* is sufficient |
| **Do not capitalise them as products** | They are stages, not modules. **Never** *Caspr. Source* as a logo lockup — logo-plus-noun is how product *lines* are named, and three of them reads as three things you buy separately |
| **Do not use *thinking*, *reasoning* or *cognitive*** anywhere near the architecture | All three are the category's own vocabulary. *Reasoning* is the worst — "reasoning models" is the exact term |
| **Do not reintroduce *brain*** | Including in passing, including internally. It is the frame we retired |

## 5 · Why these three words and no others

| | |
|---|---|
| **They are already ours** | The proof line has been a three-part architecture since it was written. **Nothing new to establish and nothing extra to defend** |
| **None is contested** | No AI company markets *assessment* or *triangulation*. **Triangulation is a named method from research methodology** — so the rigour claim has a technique behind it rather than an adjective |
| **They are true** | Each names something the product demonstrably does — and the 23-report evaluation scored exactly these: sourcing (355–678 cited claims), assessment (*"a scope-adjusted spectrum rather than a direct disagreement"*), concluding (*"should track closer to the Ken Research band"*) |
| **They divide cleanly** | A reader never has to wonder which stage does what |

> **The evaluation is worth noting here: what we scored the corpus on was the middle stage.** The Studies passed and
> the Brief failed — **on the middle stage, which is now named.** The taxonomy and the quality bar describe the
> same thing.

### Why the historical files were bannered rather than rewritten

**The leak risk is real — dated plans are exactly what a person or a session reads for context.** But a plan
written in May recording what was true in May is evidence. **Rewriting it to match an August decision destroys
the record of why the decision was made.**

**So each carries a header:** *historical, vocabulary superseded, do not copy vocabulary out of it.* **The leak
stops; the record survives.**
