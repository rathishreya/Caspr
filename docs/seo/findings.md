# SEO — what the data says

*2026-08-25. Ahrefs API, Lite plan. 830 competitor keyword rows across nine incumbents, plus volume and difficulty on 59 candidate keywords. ~37,000 of 100,000 units spent. Raw exports in [`exports/`](exports/).*

> **Read this before the website copy deck.** It kills two page families we had specced in detail, shrinks a third, and identifies one nobody had proposed that is worth more than all of them combined.

---

## 1 · Four assumptions tested. Three failed

**These were flagged as assumptions when they were made. They are now measured.**

### 1.1 ❌ `/alternatives/*` — dead. Do not build eight pages

| Query | US volume | Global |
|---|---|---|
| `ibisworld alternatives` | **0** | 10 |
| `euromonitor alternatives` | **0** | 0 |
| `mintel alternatives` | 40 | 40 |
| `statista competitors` | 40 | 70 |
| `ibisworld competitors` | 20 | 40 |
| `gartner alternatives` | 150 | 200 |
| `statista alternatives` | 100 | 200 |
| `pitchbook alternatives` | 200 | 400 |

**Roughly 490 US searches a month across the entire family**, and I proposed **eight pages** for it.

**It fails twice over.** A free SERP check on 24 August already showed `ibisworld alternatives` is owned end to end by G2, Slashdot, SourceForge, Craft and Datarade — **no vendor page ranks.** Now the volume shows there is barely anything there to win either.

**What survives:** at most **one page**, covering `pitchbook alternatives` and `statista alternatives` together (~300/mo combined). **And getting listed on G2 and Datarade almost certainly beats building it** — those are the pages that actually rank, and directory submissions are already a runbook target at 10/month.

### 1.2 ❌ The permission layer has no search volume. It is conversion content, and only that

| Query | US volume |
|---|---|
| `can i use ai for my dissertation` | **0** |
| `is it ok to use ai for research` | **0** |
| `can i use chatgpt for research` | **0** (10 global) |
| `citing ai generated content` | 10 |
| `is using ai cheating` | 90 |

**`content-approach.md` §10 claims three of these are *"acquisition content with live search volume that almost nobody credible is answering."* That was asserted with no source and it is wrong.**

**The permission layer is still worth building** — it answers the most universal objection in the persona research, it is cheap, and it never regenerates. **But it will never bring traffic.** It is linked from the ICP pages, the objection folds and the activation email, and it does its work at the point of doubt. **Reclassify it: conversion only.**

### 1.3 ⚠ The cost cluster is real but small — worth a page, not a movement

| Query | US volume | KD | **CPC** |
|---|---|---|---|
| `how much does market research cost` | 150 | **4** | **$300** |
| `market research pricing` | 150 | 2 | $350 |
| `market research cost` | 70 | 6 | **$400** |
| `cost of market research` | 60 | 10 | $200 |
| `market research report cost` | 10 | — | — |

**~440 searches a month.** Movement 3 of the editorial line gives this **two of twelve weeks**, and guerrilla rung 4 partly rests on it.

**But look at the CPC.** $300–400 a click is extraordinarily high — this is a tiny, extremely commercially valuable cluster, and **KD 2–10 means it is winnable immediately.**

**The correction is proportion, not direction.** It is worth **one strong page that owns the cluster outright**, not two weeks of an editorial calendar. `driveresearch.com` currently ranks for it and is a small agency — this is takeable.

### 1.4 ⚠ `/market-size/*` is weaker than specced

| Query | US volume | KD |
|---|---|---|
| `edtech market size` | 150 | 56 |
| `pet food market size` | 150 | 45 |
| `cybersecurity market size` | 100 | **71** |
| `cloud computing market size` | 100 | **72** |
| `electric vehicle market size` | 70 | **71** |
| `meal kit market size` | 10 | 15 |

**~100 searches a month per sector, at KD 41–72.** The big report publishers all target the `market size` modifier, so it is contested and difficult.

**The bare `[sector] market` form is a different story** — 315 such keywords in the competitor data at **median KD 6**, including `europe furniture markets` (43,000, KD 5) and `us decor markets` (28,000, KD 0). **Those two look anomalous and should be sanity-checked before anyone plans against them**, but the pattern across 315 keywords is consistent.

**So: keep the generated pages, drop the `size` framing.** And **seed narrow.** A thousand thin pages at 100 searches each is exactly the programmatic content Google penalises, and it would be a poor first impression of a company selling rigour.

## 2 · ✅ The finding nobody proposed, and it is the biggest one

**The research-methodology space is wide open, has ten times the volume of everything we planned, and carries high commercial intent.**

| Query | US volume | Global | KD | CPC |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `tam sam som` | **14,000** | 62,000 | 49 | $2 |
| `total addressable market` | **5,400** | 9,500 | 19 | $9 |
| `secondary research` | **3,400** | 12,000 | **3** | $9 |
| `market sizing` | 1,200 | 3,400 | **9** | **$140** |
| `what is secondary research` | 1,100 | 5,400 | **4** | $4 |
| `competitive landscape analysis` | 900 | 1,800 | **11** | **$300** |
| `desk research` | 450 | **6,400** | **0** | — |
| `how to do competitive analysis` | 300 | 600 | 24 | **$300** |
| `what is desk research` | 250 | 1,400 | **0** | — |
| `how to research an industry` | 150 | 200 | **8** | $170 |
| `how to size a market` | 150 | 300 | **3** | $200 |
| `industry primer` | 60 | 150 | **0** | $90 |

**Compare the totals.** Everything we planned — alternatives, cost, permission layer — comes to roughly **1,000 US searches a month.** This single family is **over 27,000**, much of it at KD 0–11.

### 2.1 And there is a live proof it can be taken

**`driveresearch.com` — a small agency — already owns most of it.** From the competitor pull, its winnable rankings include: `secondary research` (KD 0, position 4) · `mixed methods research` · `market research methods` · `primary market research` · `what is secondary research` · `desk research` · `qualitative market research` · `market research techniques` · `b2b market research companies` · `voice of customer research` · `turf analysis`.

> **A small agency with no particular authority owns the entire "how research is done" query space at KD 0–12.** That is not a moat. It is a template.

### 2.2 The tension worth naming

**The two biggest volumes — `tam sam som` (14,000) and `total addressable market` (5,400) — are founder and startup queries.** That is ICP 5, which `plan.md` §1 explicitly deprioritised on propensity to pay.

**The largest winnable search volume points at an ICP we are not targeting.** That does not overturn the ICP decision — search traffic is not the same as revenue, and founders have *"$0 for research"* (`icp-personas.md:445`). **But it should be a conscious trade rather than an accident**, and `market sizing` at KD 9 with a $140 CPC serves investors and consultants too.

## 3 · Two incumbents behave nothing like we assumed

**Statista is not a search competitor.** Its top organic traffic is `smallest country in the world`, `richest man in the world`, `trump approval rating`, `mass shootings by year`, `who won the debate`. **It runs a general-reference statistics business.** Ranking against it is neither achievable nor useful, and `/alternatives/statista` targets a query that barely exists.

**Mordor and Grand View live on the bare `[sector] market` pattern** at very low difficulty — which is the pattern worth copying, and the reason to drop the `size` modifier.

## 4 · What this changes

| | Was | Now |
|---|---|---|
| **`/alternatives/*`** | 8 pages, "highest commercial intent on the site" | **1 page at most.** Pursue G2 and Datarade listings instead |
| **Permission layer** | 6 pieces, 3 justified as acquisition | **6 pieces, all conversion.** Same build, corrected rationale |
| **Cost cluster** | Two weeks of the editorial line | **One page that owns it.** KD 2–10, CPC $300–400 |
| **`/market-size/*`** | Generated at every sector–geography | **Keep generated, drop the `size` framing, seed narrow** |
| **Methodology family** | **Did not exist in any plan** | **The primary search bet.** 27,000+/mo at KD 0–11 |

**Net effect on the twelve-week calendar:** search answers stop being spread thinly across four families and concentrate on the methodology cluster, where a zero-authority site can actually rank inside six months.
