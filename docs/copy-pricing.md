> **Four rules govern every line below.**
> **1.** No budget is written as a subscription price — *`$200 a month of research`*, never *`from $200/mo`* (`COPY-11a`).
> **2.** ⛔ **No line claims you are only charged for what you run.** `R = F + top_up`; the platform fee is charged monthly regardless of use. The claim is false (`COPY-11b`).
> **3.** ⛔ **The fee is never named, but the available balance is always shown.** Write **`$200 a month of research · ~$186 for analyses`**. Never the words *platform fee*, never `7%`, never `$14`, and no worked charge example — those live in the linked terms. `pages-for-production.md:55` · `CLAUDE.md:150–152`.
> **Why the second number is not optional:** the app's wallet header reads **`$186 available`** (`product-marketing-context` :77). A page that promises $200 and a first login that shows $186 is a trust failure on the first screen.
> **4.** No depth name without its type (`COPY-07b`). No page counts (rule 2.4).

on your own questions."*

## 2 · The Research Budget — NEW

**Eyebrow:** HOW IT WORKS
**Headline:** Set aside. Spend down. Carry the rest.

**Body:**
A research budget is money you set aside for your own research — not a fee for access, and not a seat you rent.
You choose the monthly amount. Every analysis draws against it at the price on the card below.

**Three supports:**
- **Unused balance carries forward.** It does not expire, and it is refunded in full if you cancel
- **Each month restores only what you consumed** — so a quiet month costs far less than a busy one
- **The budget is a floor, not a ceiling.** Top up whenever you need to, at any level

**Link:** Full pricing and refund terms →

**Two figures, and the cards carry both:** what you set aside, and what is available to spend on analyses.
Both appear together everywhere — cards, comparison table, wallet — and the second is never omitted.

**Source:** `pricing-model` §3.1, §7.4, §8.3, §9.1.
**Note:** **No figure appears in this fold at all.** The moment a number lands next to *"monthly"* it reads as
a bill, and the mechanics are the point here — the amounts arrive two folds later, attached to what they buy.

**⚠ Watch this fold in review.** *"Each month restores only what you consumed"* is true and permitted; the
shorter *"you are only charged for what you run"* is **false** and will be tempting to substitute. `COPY-11b`.

## 3 · What depth buys — NEW

**Eyebrow:** DEPTH
**Headline:** One level of analysis, or two, or three.

**Body:** Price follows how far the work goes, not how long the document is. Each level's ceiling is precisely
what the next one delivers.

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| **$15** | **Answers *what*.** The landscape, the players, the figures — each cited to a named source | |
| **$80** | **Adds *so what* and *now what*.** Weighs sources that disagree, reconciles them, and reaches a conclusion you can act on | |
| **$300** | **Adds validation.** Several analytical models run over the same question and checked against each other | **Coming soon** |

**Names line:** The names change with the work. Market Research runs Brief, Study and Intelligence; Investment
& Deal runs Screen, Thesis and Diligence; a business case names its rungs for the room they go into.

**Editing line:** Every analysis carries an editing allowance — enough to rewrite it twice over, restructure
sections and re-cut the charts. Further edits draw on your research budget.

**Source:** `document-taxonomy` cumulative-tier rule · `pricing-model` §4.1, §4.6 · `access-model` §1.
**Note:** **No page counts** — the corpus delivered 35–70pp against a claimed 100, and length belongs to the
topic. Levels are honest, topic-independent, and they *explain* the price gap instead of asserting it. **The
editing allowance is deliberately qualitative**: *15,000 credits* means nothing to a buyer, *rewrite it twice
over* does.

**⚠ Flag:** *"Weighs sources that disagree"* sits at **$80 and above only**. `report-evaluation` §10.4 found it
holds at Study and not at Brief, and says explicitly not to ship the unqualified claim.

## 7 · Frequently asked — EDIT

**Eyebrow:** FREQUENTLY ASKED · **Headline:** The research budget, explained.

**What is a research budget?**
Money you set aside each month for your own research. Every analysis draws against it at its listed price. It is not a fee for access and not a per-seat licence.

**What happens to what I do not use?**
It carries forward. Each month restores only what you consumed, so a quiet month costs far less than a busy one. If you cancel, the unused balance is refunded.

**Do I have to commit anything to start?**
No. Your first $100 is on Caspr, with no card. After that you can top up whenever you need to, or set a monthly budget.

**Can I change my budget?**
At any time, up or down, with a $200 floor. You can also top up mid-month whenever you need more.

**What is included in the price of an analysis?**
The analysis, every citation, the first output set in your working language, and an editing allowance large enough to rewrite it twice over.

**What costs extra?**
Outputs beyond your first set, additional languages, premium data sources, and re-generating an output after you have edited it. Each is priced and shown before you commit. **Full pricing and refund terms →**

**When does Intelligence arrive?**
Intelligence is the $300 depth — several analytical models run over the same question and checked against each other. It is not available at launch. The $15 and $80 depths cover every deliverable type today, and the $300 depth follows under each type's own name: Intelligence, Diligence, Strategic options.

**Is my data secure?**
Caspr is ISO 27001:2022 certified and GDPR compliant. Uploaded files are processed inside Caspr's own infrastructure, never reach an external AI provider, and never train any model. **Full security posture →**

**Source:** Questions drawn from `presence-metric` §4 baskets and the live `/pricing` FAQ, re-judged.
**Note:** `FAQPage` schema is for **answer engines only** — Google deprecated FAQ rich results on 7 May 2026,
so it must never be justified on those grounds. **Two answers carry the terms link**, which is where the fee
