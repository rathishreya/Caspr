This brief gives agency teams the category intelligence needed to walk into a credentials meeting fully loaded — market data, consumer segmentation, competitive positioning, and strategic frame.
Finding 1 — Growth is real but brand loyalty is structurally weak
The UK sustainable personal care category grew 34% between 2021 and 2024, but brand recall remains critically low. 61% of recent purchasers could not name the brand they bought in their most recent category purchase.

sustainable buyers with no brand recall
Finding 2 — The eco-committed segment is only 18% of buyers
Consumer segmentation reveals four distinct archetypes. The 'eco-committed' segment — willing to pay a 20%+ premium and actively seeking sustainable credentials — represents only 18% of category buyers. The largest segment (43%) is 'curious but price-sensitive.'

Finding 3 — Refill formats are underserved at retail
Refillable and concentrate formats represent 8% of shelf space but 22% of category online search volume. The gap between consumer search intent and retail availability is the clearest white space opportunity in the category.
