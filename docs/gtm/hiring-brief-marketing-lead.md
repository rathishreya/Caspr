# Hiring brief — performance marketing lead

**For the recruitment team · 2026-08-26**
**Employer: EZ · Location: Gurgaon, hybrid · Serving multiple client accounts**

---

## The role in one line

**A hands-on performance marketer who can also hold a client relationship and open a door with a publisher.**

---

## 1 · Performance marketing — the technical core

| | |
|---|---|
| **Paid and organic campaigns** | Runs them, not oversees them. **Fine-tuning to maximise return is the job**, not a quarterly review of someone else's work |
| **B2B** | Long consideration cycles, small qualified audiences, and a buyer who is rarely the user. **Not B2C volume optimisation** |
| **Markets — required** | **US and UK.** Demonstrable campaign experience, not exposure through a colleague |
| **Markets — advantage** | **Middle East and Europe** |

---

## 2 · Account management

| | |
|---|---|
| **Direct client contact** | Has owned the relationship, not sat behind someone who did |
| **Breadth across digital** | Enough command of every digital domain — paid, organic, content, email, social, analytics — to **give a client confidence that EZ can serve their whole account.** Depth in one, credible in all |
| **Presentation and communication** | Presentable in front of a client. **Moderate-to-high English proficiency** |
| **International clients** | Has worked with them directly. Comfortable across timezones and business cultures |

---

## 3 · Earned media

**The part most candidates will not have, and the part to test hardest.**

| | |
|---|---|
| **Pitching and converting** | Guest posts, podcast appearances, trade press. **Converting an editor is a relationship, not a campaign** |
| **Influencer and inclusion work** | Getting a client named in the roundups, directories and listicles that buyers and AI assistants read |
| **Why it is separate** | Performance marketing optimises a system. **This opens doors with people.** They are different muscles and rarely equally strong in one person |

---

## ⛔ Not in this role

**Brand and editorial judgement — deciding whether a piece of work is on-brand and fit to publish — sits with
the social media specialist, not this hire.** Do not screen for it or price it in.

---

## Three ways to staff this — suggestions, not a mandate

**Best case is one person who does all three. If that person does not surface quickly, either fallback is
sound.**

| | Shape | Tenure |
|---|---|---|
| **Lead** | **One person, all three** | **7–9 years** |
| **A** | **One person for 1 + 2**, freelance for earned media | **7–9 years** |
| **B** | **Two full-time** — a performance specialist, plus an account manager who also carries earned media | **4–6 years** · **5–7 years** |

### Why 7–9 and not 10+

**At ten years and beyond, people in this market have usually moved off the tools into oversight and team
leadership.** Requirement 1 asks for hands-on campaign work. **Above nine years you are usually buying
supervision of the skill rather than the skill.**

### What to watch in each fallback

| | |
|---|---|
| **A** | Freelance earned media works only if the volume is genuinely intermittent. **A steady flow of placements is better held in-house** |
| **B** | **⚠ The client relationship rests entirely on the account manager.** Five to seven years is early to hold international B2B accounts alone — screen that person harder than the specialist |
| **Lead** | **The combination is genuinely rare.** Do not let a search for it run eight weeks before concluding it is not there — run the fallbacks in parallel |

---

## ⚠ How to test — the one instruction that matters

**Test the earned media, not the performance marketing.**

**Performance is verifiable** from past accounts, platform access and real numbers. **Outreach ability is easy
to overstate and hard to check**, and it is the competence most likely to be claimed and absent.

**A concrete test that separates candidates fast.** Give them four real targets — an industry directory, a
software review site, an editorial roundup on a trade publication, and a professional forum thread where
someone has asked a genuine question.

> **A strong candidate immediately separates them:** the directory and the review site are **a form and a
> login**; the trade publication is **a pitch to a named editor**; and the forum is **neither — the only
> honest move there is to become a real participant before posting anything.**
>
> **A weak candidate calls all four "outreach."**
