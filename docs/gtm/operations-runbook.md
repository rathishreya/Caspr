## 1 · The week

One cycle, every week, same days. The whole system is designed so that a normal week requires no decisions beyond approve or reject.

| When | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **Thu 06:00** | Generation runs. Queue populates. Linter has already rejected and regenerated its own failures | Automatic |
| **Thu 07:00** | Queue-open notification: how many items, estimated minutes, deadline | Automatic |
| **Thu–Mon** | Review | DM team |
| **Mon 09:00** | One reminder to anyone with outstanding items | Automatic |
| **Mon 18:00** | **Review deadline** | — |
| **Mon 20:00** | Approved content scheduled. **Unreviewed items hold** — nothing publishes unreviewed | Automatic |
| **Tue–Sun** | Publishing runs to schedule | Automatic |
| **Tue–Sun** | Core team publishes personal posts and comments in their slots | Core team |
| **Wed 09:00** | Weekly metric snapshot lands on the dashboard | Automatic |
| **Wed 15:00** | 30-minute operations review | DM TL + Joy |

**Two notifications per week. No more.** A queue that nags gets muted, and a muted queue is a stopped machine.

## 3 · Reviewing

### The standard

You are checking whether it is **true, on-brand and worth publishing**. You are not improving it.

Three actions:

**Approve** — it ships as written.
**Reject with reason** — it regenerates immediately with your correction applied and returns to the queue.
**Hold** — you are genuinely unsure. Goes to the Wednesday review.

**There is no edit box, deliberately.** The moment reviewers rewrite, throughput collapses to the speed of writing and the ledger stops learning. If a piece is wrong, say why in ten words and the machine fixes it faster than you can.

### Reason codes

Pick the code, add a note under 200 characters. The note is the regeneration instruction, so write it as one: *"Study is $80 not $75"* rather than *"pricing wrong."*

| Code | Use when |
|---|---|
| `FACT_WRONG` | A factual claim is incorrect |
| `STALE_NUMBER` | A figure contradicts current truth |
| `UNSUPPORTED_CLAIM` | Asserted with no citable source |
| `OFF_VOICE` | Breaches brand voice — hedging, padding, hype, over-explaining |
| `BANNED_TERM` | Prohibited vocabulary. **Also flag it — the linter should have caught this** |
| `WRONG_CTA` | Wrong destination or offer |
| `DUPLICATE` | Too close to something already published |
| `LEGAL_RISK` | Regulatory or claims exposure |
| `BOUNDARY_BREACH` | Brand-separation issue |
| `WEAK` | Correct, but not worth publishing |

### What to reject on sight

- Any number you cannot match to the canonical facts panel shown beside the item
- Any claim without a source
- **"LAM" or "Large Analysis Model"** — never, under any circumstance
- **"SOC 2 certified"** — only *"SOC 2 Type I audit in progress"*
- Synthetic panels described as anything other than **AI-simulated**
- **Any reference to Ghost Research** in Caspr-published material
- An exclamation point
- Anything that would read as written by someone who had not done the work

### Time

About three minutes per item. If you are spending ten, that is a `HOLD`, not a heroic edit.

## 4 · What gets produced

Opening volume. Deliberately below capacity so there is headroom for regeneration and for scaling once the reject rate settles.

| Item | Per week | Reviewer |
|---|---|---|
| Blog posts | 2 | SEO + TL |
| LinkedIn — Joy | 1 | TL |
| LinkedIn — Jayant | 1 | TL |
| LinkedIn — rotating core team | 2–3 | Social |
| X posts | 3 | Social |
| SEO tasks — schema, internal links, metadata | 3 | SEO |
| Community thread drafts | 5 | Social |
| Outreach drafts | 4 | TL |
| Email sends | 0–2 | TL + Joy |
| **Total** | **~23 reviewed items** | **62–77 min across three people** |

Plus roughly 14 personal comments — two per person — which go straight to each individual as suggestions. **Comments do not pass through the review gate.** They are personal, low-risk, and gating them would triple the queue for no benefit.

**Revised 2026-08-24.** The earlier flat ≈70 minutes treated every item as equal. **They are not, and the difference is roughly tenfold** — reviewing origination means checking a claim, a source and a position against reality (**8–15 min**); reviewing a derivative means checking that a transformation of already-approved material stayed faithful (**1–2 min**). Only **2.5 items a week are origination**, which is the entire reason the volume fits. Full working: [`content-approach.md`](content-approach.md) §6 and [`content-calendar.md`](content-calendar.md) §1.

**Also not in this table:** the `/market-size/*` pages, generated from `fact_lookup`. **The template is reviewed once; the pages are not.**

**Scaling rule — and it now has two speeds.** Hold this volume for four weeks. If reject rate settles under 10% and review time is comfortably inside budget, increase by 25%. Repeat. Do not scale on enthusiasm.

> **Scale derivatives freely. Scale origination only when a reviewer's minutes exist for it.** Doubling item count is cheap; doubling origination is not, and the failure mode is origination creeping up because derivatives are.

**Measure the two review costs in week one and correct them here.** The 8–15 and 1–2 figures are judgement, and the whole capacity case rests on the ratio holding.

### The engagement rule

Every person: **two substantive comments per week.** One on a team post, one external.

> **No coordinated applause.** A comment adds a fact, a number, a counter-example, or a real question from your own domain. If it could have been written by someone who had not read the post, do not post it.

Five colleagues saying "Great post" is visible astroturf, and it costs a defensibility brand more than the reach is worth.

Voice lanes are in [`profile-audit.md`](../../caspr-dm-handover/02-linkedin-profiles/audit.md) §5. Never two people on the same subject in the same week.

## 7 · Paid

**Paid does not start until two conditions are met**: real testimonials published, and `/samples` holding three real reports. Until then the budget accumulates.

**Status 2026-08-26.** **The three reports are chosen** — **Saudi industrial valves** · **Reconcile UK ready meals** · **Vertical SaaS valuations** ([`../report-evaluation-2026-08.md`](../report-evaluation-2026-08.md) §6). **The gate is not open yet:** two product defects block publication, and the testimonial programme is still running. **Both conditions, not either.**

### When it starts

**Google Search first, not LinkedIn.** High commercial intent at materially lower cost per click — someone actively searching *"market research report cost"* is worth several LinkedIn impressions of someone who was not looking. LinkedIn earns its place later, for retargeting and for the two high-value ICPs search does not reach.

### Standing checklist on every campaign

Free, controllable, and it is what produces the multi-section look in a paid result:

- ☐ **Sitelink assets** — 4–6 deep links with descriptions
- ☐ **Callout assets** — *"$100 free · No credit card · Cited to source"*
- ☐ **Structured snippet assets** — *Types: Market Research, Business Case, Due Diligence, Investment Thesis*
- ☐ Landing page matches ad intent — never point a use-case ad at the homepage
- ☐ UTM parameters and `icp_hint` on every destination
- ☐ Conversion tracking confirmed firing before spend begins

### Kill rules, agreed in advance

| Trigger | Action |
|---|---|
| CAC above $300 | Pause the campaign, diagnose before resuming |
| Zero conversions after 100 clicks | Pause. The landing page or the targeting is wrong |
| CTR below 1% on search after 500 impressions | Rewrite the ad, do not raise the bid |
| Spend pacing above plan | Automatic alert; TL adjusts |

Budget changes are made by the TL and logged. No silent increases.

## 9 · The Wednesday review — 30 minutes

Same agenda every week. If it takes longer than 30 minutes, something is wrong and that is the finding.

1. **Pipeline health** — reject rate by reason, linter defects, anything held
2. **Stale flags** — published content contradicting current truth. Clear or schedule
3. **Funnel** — signups, activation, paid conversions, split by source
4. **Channel movement** — what moved, what did not
5. **Targets** — the §6 table, on track or not
6. **Paid** — spend, CAC, kill-rule triggers
7. **One decision** — the single thing needing Joy that week

## 10 · Health metrics and what to do

| Metric | Healthy | If it moves |
|---|---|---|
| **Reject rate** | 5–15% | **Above 20%:** generator miscalibrated — fix the generator, not the humans. **Below 2%:** rubber-stamping — tighten criteria and audit a sample |
| **Review completion by deadline** | 100% | Below 90% two weeks running: volume is too high. Cut it |
| **Review time per item** | 2–4 min | Rising: items are getting harder or longer. Check what changed |
| **Linter defects** | 0 | Every `BANNED_TERM` rejection is a missing rule. Add it |
| **Stale flags open** | 0 | Anything over 7 days old is a live inaccuracy on a public page |
| **Activation rate** | Rising toward 25% | The number the whole GTM is gated on |
| **Email complaint rate** | <0.1% | Above 0.3% and sending reputation is at risk |
| **CAC** | <$300 | Kill rules, §7 |

### The RoI target, stated so it can fail

**By the end of month three of operations: cumulative attributed revenue ≥ 1.5× cumulative spend, with ≥60% of it attributed to owned channels.**

If the owned-channel share comes in below half, the machine is not working and more media will not fix it.

## 11 · When things break

| Situation | Do this |
|---|---|
| Generation did not run Thursday | Portal alerts the TL. Manual trigger available. If it fails twice, the week runs at reduced volume — do not publish unreviewed content to catch up |
| Nobody reviewed by Monday 18:00 | Nothing publishes. TL decides: extend to Tuesday, or skip the week. **Skipping a week is a valid outcome.** Publishing unreviewed content is not |
| A published item is factually wrong | Unpublish first, then correct, then a `FACT_WRONG` entry so the ledger learns. In that order |
| A social token expires | Dashboard flags it. The affected person re-authorises |
| Someone leaves the company | Owner revokes access, reassigns their voice lane, and archives their queue |
| A campaign hits a kill rule | It pauses automatically. TL diagnoses before resuming |
| A truth document changes | Nothing to do. The portal absorbs it and raises stale flags on affected published content |

## 12 · What not to do

- **Do not edit content in the queue.** Reject it
- **Do not automate community posting.** Ever
- **Do not publish unreviewed content** to keep a schedule
- **Do not raise volume** because a week felt easy — follow §4
- **Do not build features during operations.** Log the request; §12 of the build spec covers when it gets built
- **Do not fix a factual error by updating the content.** Update the source document; the portal propagates it and finds every other instance
