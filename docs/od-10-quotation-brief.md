## 2 · ⚠ The rule that governs every conversation. Please read before making contact

Caspr's entire commercial position is *"every source, credible."* **A company that argues that cannot obtain
information dishonestly.** Three rules, none negotiable:

| | Rule |
|---|---|
| **1** | **Ask as who you are.** Real name, real company, real email. **No pseudonymous enquiries, no personal address to look like an individual buyer, and never a different company's identity.** If a publisher asks why we are asking, the true answer — *"we are evaluating what the market costs, and we may buy"* — is a good answer |
| **2** | **Do not manufacture purchase intent you do not have.** Asking a price is normal commercial behaviour. **Running a full sales cycle and requesting a formal proposal with no intention of buying is not** — stop at the number and the terms |
| **3** | **A number obtained in a sales conversation is not automatically publishable.** Quotes frequently arrive marked confidential. **Record it either way, flag which it is, and let Joy decide** — §6 |

> **If a conversation starts to feel like it needs a small untruth to continue, stop it and record what
> happened.** *"They would not give a figure without a procurement call"* is a perfectly good result — see §6,
> where that answer is worth more to us than the price.

### Original — what does EZ already hold?

**Start here. EZ is a buyer, and a buyer already knows the price.** If EZ subscribes to Gartner, Euromonitor,
Mintel or PitchBook, the figure already exists in your own records.

> **A number from your own procurement file is not a solicited quote. It is a fact you already own** — no sales
> conversation, no purchase intent to manufacture, no §2 question anywhere.

### ⛔ Two limits on anything EZ already owns

**Reading a report we have lawful access to is fine.** It informs us and sharpens the comparison.

**Publishing from it is a different act.** A single-user licence on a $4,750 report typically prohibits
redistribution, and often prohibits use outside the licensee's organisation entirely. **EZ's licence does not
extend to Caspr, and Caspr publishing extracts from it would breach it.**

**Also flag confidentiality.** Many enterprise contracts restrict disclosure of pricing. **If a figure is
restricted, mark it so in the workbook** and we will treat it as internal-only.

## 4 · Tab B — the comparison, from public material only

**No purchase is required.** The seller's own free product page states what a report covers, and **that is
where the comparison is made — on the question answered, not on page count.**

The Mordor *Europe Ready Meals* report costs **$4,750**, runs **110 pages**, and — per its free product page —
**segments across ten European countries.** The buyer we were testing wanted **premium ready meals in
Southeast England.**

> **$4,750 does not answer that question, and the free page proves it.** The report states what it covers. The
> buyer's question is not in it. **Nothing behind the paywall changes that.**

### What is free, and sufficient

Full tables of contents · sample pages and free abstracts · segmentation and coverage statements ·
publication date and page count. **Every syndicated seller publishes all of it, because that is how they
sell.**

### Also compare against what is free

**Not every alternative to Caspr is a $4,750 report. For most of our buyers the real alternative is what they
can assemble free** — and there is a great deal of it:

| Source | Why it is a serious comparison |
|---|---|
| **UK CMA market investigations** | **Hundreds of pages, rigorous, free.** A genuine depth benchmark |
| **ONS, Eurostat, US Census, BLS, OECD, World Bank, IMF** | The underlying data most syndicated reports repackage |
| **Trade and industry bodies** | We already cite SMMT vehicle registration data exactly this way |
| **McKinsey Global Institute, Deloitte, PwC, BCG** | Substantial, free, credible open publications |

> **This asks the question the buyer actually asks:** *"I could spend a day assembling this from free sources.
> What do I get instead?"*

## 6 · What we can publish, and what we cannot

| Always publishable | Never, without permission |
|---|---|
| **Whether a list price exists** — a fact about their website | **A confidential quote**, in any form, including as a range or "roughly" |
| **That a call was required before any number** | **A number given verbally**, unless also given in writing without restriction |
| **How long it took**, and how many touches | **A figure attributed to a named firm that they did not publish** |
| **Minimum terms and seat counts, where volunteered in writing** | **Content from a report EZ licensed** — that licence does not extend to Caspr |
| **A report's table of contents, coverage and segmentation** — the seller publishes it to sell it | Anything obtained by misdescribing who we are — which §2 prevents |
| **What free public sources do and do not answer** | **A comparison presented as independent when it is not** — below |

### Who publishes the comparison

**Caspr publishes it, in its own name, with every figure linked.**

**If EZ publishes any of it, the relationship is disclosed.** Our own buyer research records buyers
discounting **supplier-funded research as biased** — it is one of the objections Caspr exists to answer. **A
comparison published by a connected party without disclosing the connection is that exact thing**, and if the
connection were noticed later, the damage would not be to the comparison. **It would be to the one claim the
whole company rests on.**

> **The credibility cannot be borrowed. It comes from every figure carrying a link** — the same standard the
> product is sold on.

### The finding we actually want

**The most valuable output here is probably not a single price.** It is this sentence, filled in with real
numbers from Tab C:

> *"We asked nine research publishers what their product costs. **[n]** publish a price. **[n]** required a
> call before naming one. **[n]** never replied. Median time to a number: **[n]** days."*

**Every number in it comes from the workbook, none of it is confidential, and none of it is a criticism of
anyone.** It describes how the market works.

**So fill the process columns even when you get no price. A refusal is a complete result, not a failed one.**
